What this is: a week-by-week operating plan for running one pilot cohort of up to 50 caregivers.
Where it goes: internal, for Kris and anyone helping. It depends on the steps in BRAIN.md section 5 being complete. For Kris's approval. The pilot price ($7,500, proposed) and sponsor position are open decisions.

Decisions this works from (DECISIONS.md, 10 October 2026): eight-week Guided Journey; One small thing, one action a week under five minutes; eight Wheel domains; no points, streaks or badges; nothing emailed or published without Kris's approval.

# Pilot cohort run plan

## The shape of a pilot

- Up to 50 caregivers.
- A 12-week window. Caregivers start when it suits them and move through eight weeks.
- To finish inside the window, a caregiver needs to start in the first four weeks. [Decision needed: what happens to someone who starts later. Proposal: they still receive all eight weeks, and the report notes that their week-eight answers arrived after the cut-off.]
- Enrolment and delivery are run by Caregiver Wellbeing. The sponsor shares the link and receives one aggregate report.

## The automated emails

All wording is approved by Kris before the workflows are switched on. Every email carries the one-line support pathway and an unsubscribe link.

| Email | Trigger | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Intake: welcome | Caregiver enrols through the private link | Confirms enrolment, explains the eight weeks, gives the full support block, links to the first Wheel |
| Intake: Wheel nudge | First Wheel not completed after seven days | One gentle note with the link. Sent once only. |
| Weekly check-in, weeks one to eight | Seven days after the previous one, starting when the first Wheel is completed | The week's short message and one small thing. No mention of missed weeks. |
| Week-eight invite | With the week-eight check-in | Invites the second Wheel, the short wellbeing questions and two experience questions |
| Week-eight nudge | Second Wheel not completed after seven days | One note. Sent once only. |
| Closing note | Second Wheel completed, or 14 days after the week-eight invite | Thanks, what happens to their information, how to delete it, the support block |
| Report export (internal) | Window closes | De-identified data file sent to Kris. No email to caregivers or the sponsor. |

What the automation never does: count streaks, show progress percentages, send more than one nudge, mention how others are going, or email the sponsor.

## During the window

Kris's weekly routine, about 30 to 60 minutes [estimate, to be checked in the first pilot]:

- Check the workflows ran without errors.
- Read and answer the inbox.
- Note counts only: enrolled, first Wheel completed, second Wheel completed.
- Do not look at any individual's answers.

| Window week | What happens for caregivers | What Kris does | What the sponsor does |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Enrolment opens. First caregivers receive the welcome and complete the first Wheel. Week one message follows. | Check each new enrolment triggers correctly. Answer questions the same day where possible. | Shares the invitation. |
| 2 | More caregivers start. Early starters receive week two. | Check enrolment count. If under ten, talk to the sponsor about a second invitation. | Second share if agreed. |
| 3 | Rolling starts continue. | Check the Wheel nudge fired once only. | Nothing. |
| 4 | Last week to start and still finish inside the window. | Tell the sponsor the enrolment count only, if it is ten or more. Otherwise say "fewer than ten". | Final share if agreed. |
| 5 | Caregivers are between weeks two and five. | Routine. Watch for unsubscribes and note the count. | Nothing. |
| 6 | Caregivers are between weeks three and six. | Routine. Draft the report shell with participation numbers left empty. | Nothing. |
| 7 | Caregivers are between weeks four and seven. | Routine. Test the week-eight invite again before the first one sends. | Nothing. |
| 8 | Earliest starters reach week eight and receive the second Wheel invite. | Check the first week-eight responses are stored under the right codes. | Nothing. |
| 9 | More caregivers reach week eight. | Routine. Check the week-eight nudge fires once only. | Nothing. |
| 10 | More caregivers reach week eight. | Routine. | Nothing. |
| 11 | More caregivers reach week eight. | Tell the sponsor the report date. | Nothing. |
| 12 | Last caregivers who started in week four reach week eight. Window closes at the end of the week. | Close the enrolment link. Keep the week-eight form open for 14 more days for late answers. | Nothing. |

The eight weeks for each caregiver follow the same pattern: a short message, one small thing, and nothing else. The content of each week is not described here because it is not yet approved.

## Risks and what to do

| Risk | What to do |
|---|---|
| Fewer than ten caregivers enrol | Ask the sponsor to share the invitation again. Extend the start period if the agreement allows. If still under ten, report participation only and say so. Do not report outcomes. |
| Fewer than ten complete week eight | Report participation and drop-out only. Do not report the Wheel or the wellbeing measure. Tell the sponsor before the report arrives. |
| More than 50 want a place | Close the link at 50. Offer a waiting note and the register-interest page. Do not exceed the cap without the sponsor's agreement. |
| A caregiver writes in distress | Reply the same working day with the full support block: Carer Gateway 1800 422 737, Lifeline 13 11 14, 000 in an emergency, and their GP. Do not give advice or counselling. Keep a private note that a support reply was sent, with no detail. |
| A caregiver writes outside working hours in distress | The automatic reply on the inbox carries the support numbers and says when email is read. Set this up before the window opens. |
| A sponsor asks who enrolled, or asks for a breakdown | Decline, and point to the agreement. Offer the aggregate figure if it is ten or more. |
| A sponsor asks to add a message, logo or product mention | Decline, and point to the agreement. The disclosure line is the only sponsor wording caregivers see. |
| An employer cohort is small enough that people could be guessed | Report no breakdowns at all. If the whole cohort is under ten completers, participation only. |
| A workflow fails or sends twice | Pause the workflow. Fix and test. Send one short, plain apology if caregivers were affected. Record what happened. |
| An email goes to the wrong person, or data is exposed | Follow the privacy notice: tell the people affected, tell them what is being done, and take advice from the lawyer on notification duties. |
| The measure's licence is not confirmed in time | Do not use it. Run the pilot with the Wheel and the two experience questions, and say so in the report. |
| The eight weeks are not approved in time | Do not open the window. Move the dates and tell the sponsor plainly. |
| Kris is unavailable for a week | A named person reads the inbox and uses the reply templates. Workflows continue. Nothing new is published. |
| Results are flat or negative | Report them as they are. Say what will change. Do not soften the figures. |
| A caregiver asks for deletion mid-cohort | Delete within the promised time. Remove them from the workflows first so no more emails send. |
