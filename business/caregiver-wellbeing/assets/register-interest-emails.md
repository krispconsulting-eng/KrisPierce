What this is: three short emails for caregivers who register interest on the Wellbeing page, sent on day 0, day 7 and day 21.
Where it goes: the automated email sequence, once Kris approves the wording and the consent wording is settled. Nothing is sent before then.

Notes:

- Each email is under 110 words, not counting the subject line and footer.
- They contain no content from the unapproved lessons and no definitions of the eight areas.
- No dates are promised, because none are decided.
- Sender name: Kris at Caregiver Wellbeing. Reply-to: info@krispierce.com.au.

Footer for every email:

> Caregiver Wellbeing is operated by Kris Pierce Consulting. You are receiving this because you registered your interest. Unsubscribe: [link].
> Need support now? Carer Gateway 1800 422 737. Lifeline 13 11 14. In an emergency, call 000.
