# Caregiver Wellbeing: organisation licence

Space for YOU

Twelve months of access to an eight-week Guided Journey for the unpaid caregivers in your workforce or community. Operated by Kris Pierce Consulting.

## Why it matters

- About 3.0 million Australians are unpaid caregivers, almost 12% of people living in households. Source: Australian Bureau of Statistics, Survey of Disability, Ageing and Carers 2022.
- 61.1% of caregivers have low wellbeing, compared with 33.6% of Australian adults. Source: 2025 Carer Wellbeing Survey, University of Canberra for Carers Australia.

Some of these people work for you, or are the families of the people you serve.

## What caregivers get

- The Wheel: a short reflection on their own life across eight areas. A reflection, never a score.
- Eight weeks of one small thing a week, each under five minutes.
- A second Wheel at week eight.
- Free to them, private, self-paced. No points, streaks or pressure.

## What the licence includes

- Twelve months of access for your caregivers.
- A private enrolment link for you to share.
- Two aggregate reports a year.
- A launch session for staff.

## What you receive, and what you do not

You receive aggregate reports only. No figure is shown for any group smaller than ten people.

You do not receive names, individual results, or anything that could identify a staff member, client or family member. You are never told who enrolled. You have no say over the content, and no product or promotional message reaches caregivers. These terms are in the agreement.

## What it is not

Caregiver Wellbeing is a non-clinical wellbeing programme. It does not diagnose, treat, prevent or screen for any condition. It does not replace an employee assistance programme, counselling or Carer Gateway. It is informed by evidence, and each report states its limits.
