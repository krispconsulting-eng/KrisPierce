# 3. The Wheel

Shown on the Wheel start screen, at week one and again at week eight.

## Before you begin

The Wheel is a quiet look at your own life across eight areas. It takes about [number] minutes.

- There are no right answers. What you see at the end is a reflection, not a score.
- Your answers are about you, so we treat them as sensitive information.
- We also ask [number] short questions about your wellbeing. You will not see a result for these. We use them only to understand how the whole group is going.
- Your answers are stored under a private code, apart from your name and email.
- You can skip any question.

## How your answers are used

- To show you your own reflection.
- Combined with other caregivers' answers, with names removed, to see what changed for the group.
- In a group report for the organisation that funded the places. The report never shows a group smaller than ten people, and never shows any one person's answers.

## Tick boxes

[ ] I agree that Caregiver Wellbeing can collect and keep my Wheel answers to show me my reflection. (required to use the Wheel)

[ ] I agree that my answers can be combined with other caregivers' answers, with names removed, for group reports. (optional) [Lawyer to advise whether this can be optional in a sponsored cohort, and what happens to reporting if many people decline.]

# 5. The sponsor-data promise

For caregiver-facing pages, and repeated in the sponsor agreement.

## Caregiver-facing version

Organisations fund places so that you never pay. Here is what they get, and what they do not.

They get one report about the whole group. It shows how many people took part and what changed overall.

They do not get:

- your name or email
- your answers or your reflection
- any hint of whether you enrolled
- any figure for a group smaller than ten people
- any say over what is in the programme
- any way to advertise to you

This is written into our agreement with every organisation.

## Agreement version

1. The sponsor receives aggregate reports only.
2. No figure is reported for a group of fewer than ten people.
3. The sponsor receives no names, contact details, individual responses or enrolment status for any person.
4. Where the sponsor is an employer, the sponsor receives nothing that could identify a staff member, including by team, site or role.
5. The sponsor has no control over programme content, participants or findings.
6. No product name, promotion or sponsor message is shown or sent to caregivers.
7. The sponsor's support is disclosed in plain words wherever the cohort is described.
8. Kris Pierce Consulting is a business. It is not a patient organisation or a health consumer organisation.

# 6. Stopping, withdrawing and deleting

## Line for every email footer

You can stop at any time. Unsubscribe here: [link]. To have your information deleted, write to info@krispierce.com.au.

## Screen or page: stopping

You can stop whenever you like. You do not need to tell us why, and nothing bad happens.

- To stop the emails, use the unsubscribe link in any email.
- To leave the programme and keep your information for now, do nothing more.
- To leave and have your information deleted, write to info@krispierce.com.au with the words "delete my information".

## Reply sent when someone asks for deletion

Hello [first name],

We have received your request. We will delete your name, email address and answers within [number] days and write once more to confirm it is done.

Group results that were already combined, and that cannot identify you, stay as they are. They contain nothing that points to you.

You are welcome back at any time.

Kris
Caregiver Wellbeing
info@krispierce.com.au

Hello [first name],

Your information has been deleted. This is the last email you will receive from us.

Take care.

Kris
