# 1. Carer Gateway provider

Subject: A digital wellbeing component for caregivers

Hello [first name],

I run Caregiver Wellbeing, an eight-week online Guided Journey for unpaid caregivers. It is free for caregivers and funded by organisations.

It is deliberately small. A caregiver reflects on eight areas of their own life, does one small thing a week, then reflects again at week eight. It is non-clinical and sits beside your coaching and counselling.

In the 2025 Carer Wellbeing Survey (University of Canberra for Carers Australia), 61.1% of caregivers had low wellbeing, against 33.6% of Australian adults.

I am looking for three organisations to fund a pilot cohort of up to 50 caregivers in 2027. You would receive an aggregate report and no individual data.

Would a 30-minute conversation be useful as you plan for July 2027?
