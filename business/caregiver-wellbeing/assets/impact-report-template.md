What this is: the template for the aggregate impact report a sponsor or licence holder receives after a cohort.
Where it goes: filled in by Kris after each cohort, laid out in the Natural Coastal v1.2 brand, and sent to the sponsor's contact person. Depends on the measurement plan being approved.

Rules for whoever fills this in:

- Every cell in square brackets is a placeholder. Never estimate a figure. If it is not known, write "not collected".
- No figure is shown for a group of fewer than ten people. See section 7.
- No quotes from caregivers unless that person gave written consent for that exact wording.
- No names, initials, job titles, locations or any detail that could point to a person.
- The Wheel is reported as reflections for the group. No totals, averages per person, rankings or scores.

## 7. How we did this

Plain-language method note:

- Caregivers enrolled themselves through a private link. No one was selected or screened.
- Each caregiver completed the Wheel and a short wellbeing measure at the start. Those who reached week eight were invited to complete both again, with two short questions about the experience.
- Answers at the two points were matched by a private code, then names and email addresses were removed before analysis.
- We compared the group at week one with the same group at week eight.
- The Wheel was analysed as group patterns for each area. No totals or individual scores were made.
- Analysis was done by [name and role]. [State whether anyone independent checked it.]

Minimum group size rule:

- No number, percentage or average is reported for a group of fewer than ten people.
- Small groups are merged with a neighbouring group, or shown as "fewer than ten".
- Where a hidden number could be worked out by subtracting other numbers in the report, a second number is also hidden.
- If fewer than ten caregivers completed both points, sections 3, 4 and 5 are not reported. The report then contains participation only.
- No breakdown is given by workplace team, site, role, age, gender, postcode or the person being cared for, unless every group has ten or more people and the sponsor agreement allows it.

## 8. Limits and caveats

- There was no comparison group. These results describe change. They do not show cause.
- People chose to take part. They may differ from caregivers who did not.
- People who finished may differ from people who stopped. See section 6.
- The group is small. A different group could give different results.
- Eight weeks is short. We do not know whether any change lasts.
- All answers are self-reported.
- Other things were happening in people's lives during these weeks.
- Caregiver Wellbeing is a non-clinical programme. Nothing here is a clinical finding about any person or group.
