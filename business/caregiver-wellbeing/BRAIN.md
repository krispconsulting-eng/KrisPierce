## 1. The business in one paragraph

Three million Australians care for someone without pay, and six in ten of them report low wellbeing. Caregiver Wellbeing gives a caregiver a quiet way to look at their own life across eight areas, then eight weeks of one small thing at a time. The caregiver never pays. Organisations that have a reason to care about caregivers, as employers, service providers, funders or supporters, pay for places and receive an aggregate report on what changed.

## 2. Why sponsor-funded, and why now

- The need is measured: 61.1% of carers had low wellbeing in the 2025 Carer Wellbeing Survey, against 33.6% of Australian adults, and the gap grew.
- Caregivers will not and should not pay. Carer Gateway coaching and counselling are free, so a paid consumer course competes with free. The July plan's $97 self-pay price is better dropped.
- Organisations do pay for carer support. UK councils commission digital carer support at about £36,000 for a pilot to £73,500 a year. Employer programmes cost £1,200 to £2,500 a year.
- 2027 has four buying moments: Carer Gateway providers re-tender for 1 July 2027; Victoria's Support for Carers round covers 2027 to 2031; the first National Carer Strategy action plan ends in December 2027; Carers Week falls in October.

Sources in `../research/02-caregiver-wellbeing-market.md`.

## 3. Customers (the payers) and users (the caregivers)

The user is any unpaid caregiver. The customer is one of these:

| Payer | Why they buy | Likely first purchase |
|---|---|---|
| Carer Gateway providers and state carer organisations | A distinct digital component for their 2027 bids and services | Pilot cohort, then a licence |
| Employers with a carer commitment (Carers + Employers accredited, or with carer leave policies) | Something practical to offer staff who are caregivers | Organisation licence |
| Disability and aged care providers, hospitals | Support for the families of the people they serve | Sponsored cohort |
| Patient organisations, with a funder behind them | A wellbeing offer for their caregiver members | Sponsored cohort |
| Companies and foundations supporting carers | Unrestricted, reportable community support | Sponsored cohort |
| State governments | Carer wellbeing programmes (South Australia and Western Australia funded these in 2026-27) | Grant-funded delivery with a partner |

First approach: two or three Carer Gateway bidders and two patient organisations Kris already knows. They move fastest and give the pilot data that everyone else will ask for.

| **For caregivers** | The Wheel, the eight-week Guided Journey, one small thing each week, a second Wheel at week eight | Free, always |

| **Sponsored cohort** | As above, after the pilots | $12,000 for 50 places ($240 a place); $21,000 for 100 |
| **Organisation licence** | Twelve months of access for an organisation's caregivers, a private enrolment link, two aggregate reports a year, a launch session for staff | $9,000 (up to 100 caregivers); $18,000 (up to 500); regional or provider-wide by quote, $40,000 to $75,000 |
| **Community places** | Any amount funds places in an open pool that caregivers apply to | $200 a place (the July figure) |

How this relates to the July model: the $200 seat survives as the community place. Tiers by seat count are replaced by cohorts and licences, because buyers budget for programmes, not seats.

What sponsors get, and do not get: an aggregate report (minimum group size ten), acknowledgement in plain words, and nothing else. No names, no individual results, no say over content, no product presence. This is written into the agreement.

## 6. Strategy

**Position.** The low-burden option. Everything else asks caregivers to do more; this asks for one small thing. For payers: a structured programme with a report, at a fraction of a commissioned service.

**How it wins.** Kris's 20 years as a carer, a method that respects the caregiver's time, and a report payers can use. The discipline of the brand (no guilt, no streaks, no scores) is the product.

**Evidence stance.** Say "informed by evidence". Digital programmes for caregivers show short-term reductions in stress and burden in meta-analyses; no trial has tested this exact format. The pilots are how the evidence gets built, with honest reporting including drop-out.

**Path to scale.** Pilots in 2027 produce data. Data supports licences in 2028. A licence held by a Carer Gateway provider or a large employer is repeat revenue with little of Kris's time. Later options, only with evidence in hand: a facilitator licence for organisations to run groups, and versions for other audiences (the gamified code is held in reserve for those).

**Twelve-month targets (base case).** Two pilot cohorts, one full cohort, one organisation licence: about $39,000, 150 to 200 caregivers reached, a published pilot report at Carers Week 2027.

## 9. Voice and design system (locked, reuse only)

Authority: `caregiver-wellness-course/BRAND-BRIEF.md` v2. That file wins over anything here.

- Voice: calm, plain, human, practical. Short sentences. Speak to "you". Sentence case. No exclamation marks, emoji, guilt, urgency, countdowns, streaks or scores. "Space for YOU", "caregiver", "One small thing".
- Colour: Deep ocean #14384A (text, primary buttons), Deep natural #4D7765 (links), Coastal mist #A8C9D0, Soft sand #EADBC8, Warm paper #F8F7F2, White. No black, gradients or glows.
- Wheel palette: the eight confirmed domain colours, on white, always with a labelled list.
- Type: Newsreader (headings) and Hanken Grotesk (everything else).
- Shape: rectangles and rounded rectangles; no blobs, waves or arches. Logo: supplied files only.
- Payer-facing documents use the same system, with the line "Operated by Kris Pierce Consulting".

## 10. Legal and compliance checklist

- [ ] Privacy: the small business exemption is unlikely to apply because wellbeing answers are health information. Full Australian Privacy Principles compliance, explicit consent, data held in Australia where practical, deletion on request.
- [ ] Non-clinical positioning held in every line of copy: no diagnosing, treating, preventing or screening. "Common experiences, not a diagnosis. If you're worried, talk to your GP."
- [ ] A support pathway on every screen where someone may be struggling: Carer Gateway 1800 422 737, Lifeline 13 11 14, and 000 in an emergency.
- [ ] Sponsor agreement: unrestricted, disclosed, aggregate reporting only, minimum group of ten, no content control. Employer sponsors receive nothing that could identify a staff member.
- [ ] Medicines Australia Code position for company sponsors decided; agreement states that Kris Pierce Consulting is a business, not a patient organisation.
- [ ] Measurement tool licence confirmed in writing before use.
- [ ] Photo source, licence and consent recorded for every image.
- [ ] Third-party material (for example the NAC and Global Genes guidebook) not reproduced.
- [ ] No testimonial, story or statistic that is not real, consented and labelled.
- [ ] Trade mark search: "Caregiver Wellbeing" is descriptive and may not register alone; consider the logo and "Space for YOU".
