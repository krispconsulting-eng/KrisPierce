## Email 2: Day 2

**Subject:** The prompt that fixes most bad AI drafts

Hi [first name],

Most disappointing AI drafts have the same cause: the assistant was told what to write and nothing about who it is for.

Try adding these four lines to the top of your next request:

1. Who we are: "We are a small Australian patient organisation for [condition group]."
2. Who will read this: "Parents and carers, many new to the diagnosis."
3. What good looks like: "Plain language, short sentences, no promises about treatment."
4. What to do when unsure: "If you are not sure of a fact, say so. Do not guess."

That last line matters most. AI assistants can state invented facts with complete confidence, and line 4 reduces it without removing it. You still check everything before it leaves the building.

Save the four lines somewhere you can copy them from. That is your first reusable prompt.

Kris
