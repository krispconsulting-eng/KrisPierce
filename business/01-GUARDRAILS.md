# Guardrails: conflicts, compliance and ownership

Every offer, page and email in this folder is written to sit inside these rules. If a later edit breaks one, treat it as a defect.

Status: drafted by Claude Code, 15 November 2026. Not legal advice. Items marked "Kris decides" are listed again in `DECISIONS-FOR-KRIS.md`.

## 1. Public appointments

Kris holds public roles: Deputy Chair of the HTA Consumer Consultative Committee, Member of the MSAC Evaluation Sub-Committee, and Member of the Genomics Australia Advisory Council.

Rules applied throughout this folder:

1. No commercial page, deck, bio or email lists these roles as a reason to buy. Where a bio needs them for honesty, they appear once, in a plain "Other roles and declarations" line, with the sentence: "These are public appointments. Kris does not represent these bodies in any consulting work, and clients receive no access to them or to their deliberations."
2. No offer includes product-specific work on an application that is, or is likely to be, before MSAC, PBAC or a committee Kris sits on or advises. That excludes drafting, reviewing or coaching on a sponsor's submission, and advising a sponsor on how a committee is likely to respond.
3. What stays in scope: teaching how the Australian system works from public sources, building an organisation's general engagement capability, designing consumer evidence methods that are not tied to a live application, and work for patient organisations, researchers and government.
4. Every proposal carries a conflict check step before acceptance (template in `kpc-engagement/assets/proposal-template.md`).

Finding to resolve (Kris decides): the live consulting site's "Policy and HTA engagement" page currently offers sponsors "submission review and advisory through the assessment process". That sits uneasily beside rule 2. Options are to remove that line, to keep it and manage it through each committee's declaration and recusal process, or to limit it to technologies outside the committees' remit. My recommendation is to remove it from public copy and decide case by case with the committee secretariats, because the reputational cost of a perceived conflict is larger than the fee from any one engagement.

## 4. Medicines Australia Code of Conduct

Applied to anything pharma-adjacent:

- No product promotion, no product names in public content, no comparative claims.
- Sponsorship of Caregiver Wellbeing, Rare and Raw or a Rare Intelligence workshop is unrestricted and arm's length: the sponsor has no say over content, speakers, participants or findings, and that is written into the agreement.
- Sponsors never receive individual participant data. Reports are aggregate, with a minimum cell size of ten.
- Sponsor support is disclosed in plain words wherever the supported activity appears.
- Fees paid to Kris by a company are at fair market value for work done, under a written agreement, and can be declared wherever those roles require it.
- Companies report their support of health consumer organisations publicly. Kris Pierce Consulting is a business, not a health consumer organisation, so sponsorship agreements say so plainly to avoid misreporting.
- Sponsored content is never directed at getting consumers to ask for a prescription medicine.

## 5. Health claims and privacy

- Nothing here is medical advice, and every public page says so once, plainly.
- Caregiver Wellbeing is a non-clinical wellbeing program. It does not diagnose, treat or screen. Results are reflections, never scores. Copy states its limits: "Common experiences, not a diagnosis. If you're worried, talk to your GP."
- Wellbeing answers are sensitive information under the Privacy Act. Collect the minimum, get explicit consent, store in Australia where practical, and never pass identifiable answers to a sponsor or employer.
- Rare Intelligence teaches AI use. It tells clients not to put identifiable patient or family information into general AI tools, and its own tools hold none.
- No invented people, testimonials, statistics or case studies anywhere. Where a real one is missing, the slot says what is needed and stays empty.
