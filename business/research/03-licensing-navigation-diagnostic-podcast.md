# Research notes: lived experience licensing, navigation, engagement diagnostic, podcast

Compiled 15 November 2026 from web search summaries. Page fetches were blocked, so figures come from search-result extracts. Check the primary source before quoting.

## Lived Experience Network licence

- Amaze: organisations ask Amaze for lived experience input and Amaze approaches matching network members; expressions of interest opened November 2025. No fee to organisations or member pay rate found. https://www.amaze.org.au/get-involved/lived-experience/ https://www.amaze.org.au/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Terms-of-Reference-Amaze-Lived-Experience-Nework.pdf
- Adviser remuneration in Australia: NSW Health $40 an hour, $60 to chair; Health Consumers Queensland $50 an hour for one-off work; ANU $50 an hour; Safer Care Victoria up to $251 a day; WAAMH $35 an hour. https://www.health.nsw.gov.au/patients/experience/Pages/remuneration-consumers.aspx https://www.anu.edu.au/news/all-news/fair-recognition-for-health-care-consumers-involved-in-research https://waamh.org.au/assets/policies/Lived-Experience-Participation-Payments.pdf
- Licensing precedent: Mental Health First Aid Australia instructors sign a licence agreement and pay an annual fee (amount not found); instructor training for The Working Mind is $5,450 plus GST from 1 July 2026. https://mhfainstructors.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/14310066097679-Becoming-a-TWM-Instructor-FAQs
- Legal risk: a licence with fees, training and royalties can be treated as a franchise under the Franchising Code. Get advice before selling one. https://legalvision.com.au/franchise-licence

## Care navigation pathway

- Rare Voices Australia: $2.48 million for the Navigator Project (RARE Helpline, telehealth nurse navigation), July 2023 to June 2026. No continuation funding found. https://rarevoices.org.au/grant-update-rare-and-complex-disease-telehealth-nurse-program/
- RArEST: $1.9 million to UNSW in 2021. https://www.unsw.edu.au/newsroom/news/2021/07/funding-supports-australians-living-with-a-rare-disease-
- Cancer analogue: $453 million since November 2023 for the Australian Cancer Nursing and Navigation Program. https://www.health.gov.au/our-work/australian-cancer-nursing-and-navigation-program
- Reading: navigation is funded by Commonwealth grants to peak bodies and universities, and the outputs are published free. No priced, licensed pathway product was found. It works as a funded project, not a product.

## Engagement diagnostic, co-design training, AI evaluation

- Existing Australian maturity tools are free: Safety and Quality Commission Action 2.11 guidance and self-assessment tool; Safer Care Victoria Partnering in healthcare tool. https://www.safetyandquality.gov.au/node/3626 https://safetyandquality.gov.au/sites/default/files/2019-06/self-assessment-tool-person-centred-healthcare-organisations.pdf
- No commercially priced consumer engagement maturity tool was found. The saleable part is interpretation and follow-on work, not the instrument.
- Co-design training: TACSI Foundations for Co-Design $2,145 per person; TACSI in-house $16,500 for up to 10 people, $33,000 for up to 25; Fundamentals of Co-Design $935; Co-Design in Complex Systems $990; practitioner bootcamp $3,960. https://learn.tacsi.org.au/courses/foundations-for-codesign https://learn.tacsi.org.au/courses/foundations-for-co-design-for-organisations https://events.humanitix.com/fundamentals-of-co-design-2025
- AI evaluation by domain experts: Mercor medical listings US$60 to $180 an hour in 2026; these are clinician figures. No rate for consumer engagement evaluators was found. https://work.mercor.com/m/V685V856

## Podcast

- Buzzsprout 2026 benchmarks: median episode about 27 to 28 downloads in seven days; top quarter 97; top tenth 409. https://buzzsprout.com/stats
- Host-read sponsorship: US$25 to $40 per thousand downloads (Acast). https://www.acast.com/news-and-insights/how-much-does-podcast-advertising-cost
- Arithmetic: 400 downloads at a $30 CPM is $12 an episode. CPM pricing does not work at niche scale; flat-fee season sponsorship is the only workable model.
- Video: YouTube is the most-used podcast platform for about 40% of US listeners; 44% of podcast consumers watched a video podcast in the past week. https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/414493/
- Pharma sponsors can fund a show with acknowledgement but cannot have product content or editorial control (Medicines Australia Code Edition 20; clause wording not retrieved).
