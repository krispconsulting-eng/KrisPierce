# Research notes: caregiver wellbeing market

Compiled 15 November 2026 from web search summaries. Page fetches to several sites were blocked, so figures come from search-result extracts. Check the primary source before quoting. "Unverified" means not confirmed in this session.

## Size and need

- 3.0 million unpaid carers in Australia, almost 12% of people living in households; 1.2 million primary carers, up from 861,600 in 2018 (ABS SDAC 2022). https://abs.gov.au/media-centre/media-releases/55-million-australians-have-disability https://www.aihw.gov.au/reports/australias-welfare/informal-carers
- 2025 Carer Wellbeing Survey (University of Canberra for Carers Australia, 10,918 responses): 61.1% of carers have low wellbeing, up from 57.7% in 2024, against 33.6% of Australian adults. https://www.carersaustralia.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/CWS25_Report_231025_.pdf
- 2018 SDAC employment-to-population ratios: primary carers 55.8%, other carers 71.9%, non-carers 77.4%. https://dataresearch.ndis.gov.au/media/3951/download
- National Carer Strategy 2024-2034: first action plan runs to December 2027; $907 million over four years to 2027-28 cited for carer support services overall. https://www.health.gov.au/resources/publications/national-carer-strategy-action-plan-2024-2027 https://ministers.dss.gov.au/media-releases/17081
- Carer Gateway: provider agreements end 30 June 2027; new grant opportunity (GO8455) from 1 July 2027. Services are free to carers. https://www.health.gov.au/sites/default/files/2026-07/integrated-carer-support-service-carer-gateway-service-providers-grant-opportunity-webinar-slides.pdf
- Budget 2026-27: Carer-Inclusive Workplace Initiative extended, $3.2 million over four years. https://www.carersaustralia.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Budget-2026-27-What-it-means-for-carers_Final.pdf
- National Carers Week 2027: likely 10 to 16 October (unverified; 2026 was 11 to 17 October).

## Evidence and measures

- Internet-based interventions for caregivers (Sherifali et al., 2018, JMIR meta-analysis): reduced stress and distress (-0.48) and anxiety (-0.40); many studies at high risk of bias. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29970358
- Digital interventions, 2025 meta-analysis: short-term reductions in burden (d = -0.65) and stress (d = -0.62). https://public-pages-files-2025.frontiersin.org/journals/digital-health/articles/10.3389/fdgth.2025.1636084/pdf
- Compassion-based trial (Denmark, 161 caregivers): improvements in depression, anxiety and stress held at six months. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7941195
- No trial of the exact "one small action a week" format was found. Say "informed by evidence", never "proven".

## Compliance

- Privacy Act: the small business exemption does not apply to an organisation that provides a health service and holds health information, and "health service" includes assessing or improving psychological health. Plan for full Australian Privacy Principles compliance. https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/the-privacy-act/rights-and-responsibilities
- TGA: general health or wellness software can be excluded from medical device regulation, depending on the intended purpose shown in marketing. https://www.tga.gov.au/resources/guidance/understanding-general-health-or-wellness-software-exclusion
- Avoid any claim to diagnose, treat, prevent or screen for a condition.
- National Safety and Quality Digital Mental Health Standards are voluntary and aimed at mental health services; a general wellbeing programme appears out of scope, though government buyers may ask about alignment. https://www.safetyandquality.gov.au/standards/national-safety-and-quality-digital-mental-health-standards

## What this means

1. The need is large and measured: 61.1% of carers with low wellbeing against 33.6% of adults.
2. Free government services set the floor. The paid product is the structured journey plus the sponsor's aggregate report.
3. UK council contracts for digital carer support (about £36,000 for a pilot to £73,500 a year) are the clearest price comparable. Employer memberships sit far lower.
4. Timing favours 2027: Carer Gateway re-tender for 1 July 2027, Victoria's 2027 to 2031 round, action plan ending December 2027, Carers Week in October.
5. Use a free measure. The Personal Wellbeing Index fits the national survey's measure family (confirm licence before use).

Estimated price corridor (my reasoning from the comparators, not a sourced figure): a sponsored cohort of 50 caregivers at AUD 7,500 to 15,000; a first pilot at AUD 5,000 to 7,500; an annual licence at AUD 6,000 to 12,000 for small and mid-size organisations, 15,000 to 30,000 for large ones, and 40,000 to 75,000 for a region-wide provider licence.
