## Section copy

**Overline:** Ways to work with me

**Heading:** Start free. Go only as far as is useful.

**Intro:** You are expected to show how consumers shaped your decisions. There are six ways to work with me, and they run in order: a free diagnostic, a fixed-price debrief, then the work that fits what you find. Every offer has a fixed scope and a published price. You can stop at any step, or start at the one that fits.

All prices are in Australian dollars and exclude GST.

### The path

**1. CEOF Diagnostic**
See where your engagement stands across seven domains, in about ten minutes.
**Free**
[Take the free diagnostic](/diagnostic)

**2. Diagnostic Debrief**
A 90-minute working session on your results, and a written 90-day action plan within five working days.
**$1,500**, credited against a sprint booked within 60 days
[Book a debrief](/advisory/diagnostic-debrief)

**3. Strategic Advisory Sprint**
Four weeks on one decision, ending with a decision paper and a leadership briefing.
**$12,000**
[About the sprint](/advisory/strategic-advisory-sprint)

**4. Consumer Engagement Design**
Discovery, co-design sessions with consumers, synthesis, and a framework your organisation can run.
**Focused $18,000. Standard $30,000. Full $45,000.** Consumer payments are at published rates and costed separately.
[About engagement design](/advisory/consumer-engagement-design)

**5. Ongoing Strategic Advisor**
A retained adviser: a monthly working session, document review and on-call advice.
**$3,500 a month** (1.5 days) or **$6,500 a month** (3 days). Six-month minimum.
[About ongoing advice](/advisory/ongoing-strategic-advisor)

**6. In-house co-design training**
One day for up to 12 people, with a workbook and a follow-up call 30 days later.
**$9,500**
[About the training day](/advisory/co-design-training)

After a sprint, the path divides. Choose design when the work needs consumers in the room, an ongoing arrangement when you need advice across many decisions, or training when your own team should be doing it.

### Charity rate

Organisations with annual revenue under $1 million receive 25% off the Strategic Advisory Sprint and the in-house co-design training. Places are limited each quarter.

### Out of scope

I do not take on product-specific submission work for any health technology assessment committee. Every proposal goes through a conflict check before it is accepted.
