## H1

CEOF Diagnostic

## Lede

See where your consumer engagement stands, in about ten minutes, before you speak to anyone.

## Who it is for

Patient organisations, research teams, companies and agencies that are expected to show how consumers shaped their decisions, and want an honest read of where they stand first.

## What you get

- An overall maturity level, from Ad hoc to Community-led
- A result for each of the seven domains
- Your three priority domains, each with a first move
- A written report sent to you by email

In detail:

- The Community Engagement Operating Framework (CEOF) Diagnostic: about 28 statements across seven domains
- An instant on-screen result with your overall level: Ad hoc, Emerging, Structured, Embedded or Community-led
- A result for each domain: Purpose and Governance; Voice and Representation; Capability and Culture; Engagement Practice; Safety, Ethics and Recognition; Feedback and Learning; Outcomes and Impact
- Three priorities, drawn from your three lowest domains, with a first move for each
- A written report by email

## How it runs

You rate about 28 statements across seven domains. Your result appears on screen as soon as you finish, and the written report follows by email. There is no call to book and nothing to pay.

1. **Answer.** Rate about 28 statements about how your organisation involves consumers. It takes about ten minutes.
2. **See your result.** Your overall level, seven domain results and three priorities appear on screen straight away.
3. **Read your report.** A written report arrives by email, with a first move for each priority.
4. **Decide what is next.** Act on the first moves yourself, or book a Diagnostic Debrief to turn the result into a 90-day plan.

## Why it matters

Most organisations cannot say where their engagement is strong and where it is thin. A result you can read in ten minutes gives the next conversation something specific to work from.

## Price

**Free**

No payment details and no call required.

**Time:** About ten minutes

## What is not included

- A conversation with me about the results. That is the Diagnostic Debrief.
- An audit or accreditation. The result reflects your own answers.
- Advice on any product-specific submission to a health technology assessment committee. I do not offer that work.

The person closest to how engagement runs in your organisation. It works well completed with one or two colleagues in the room, so the answers reflect more than one view.

## H1

Strategic Advisory Sprint

## Lede

Four weeks on one decision, ending with a paper your leadership team can act on.

## Who it is for

Leaders and teams with one engagement decision in front of them and a date by which it has to be made.

## What you get

- A clear read on where the decision stands now
- Options set out, with a recommendation and its rationale
- A written decision paper
- A briefing to your leadership team

In detail:

- Four weeks of my time on one decision: a consumer evidence plan, an engagement strategy, a trial-readiness review or a partnership approach
- A conflict check before the proposal is accepted
- Targeted conversations with the people who hold the decision
- Review of the documents that bear on it
- A written decision paper
- A briefing to your leadership team

## How it runs

A short, structured engagement on one decision. Targeted conversations, focused analysis, and a written paper. No long discovery cycles. No padded deliverables.

1. **Week one: frame the decision.** You and I agree the one decision, who owns it, and what the paper has to answer.
2. **Week two: gather what bears on it.** I read the relevant documents and speak with the people closest to the decision.
3. **Week three: test the options.** I set out the options and test a draft recommendation with you.
4. **Week four: decide.** You receive the decision paper, and I brief your leadership team.

## Why it matters

When direction is unclear, decisions stall. A sprint puts one decision in front of the people who own it, with what they need to make it.

## H1

Consumer Engagement Design

## Lede

Engagement approaches that influence decisions and withstand scrutiny.

## Who it is for

Organisations that need consumer engagement to be more than a box-tick: engagement that holds up under regulatory, clinical, and policy scrutiny.

## What you get

- An engagement approach designed for the specific decision context
- Co-design sessions with consumers, run by me
- A synthesis of what was heard and what it means for the decision
- A framework your organisation can run without me

In detail:

- Discovery: the decisions the engagement has to inform and who needs to be involved
- Co-design sessions with consumers
- Synthesis of what was heard, written so it can be used in a decision
- A framework your organisation can run
- A conflict check before the proposal is accepted
- Three sizes. Focused: one program, project or grant; 4 discovery interviews, 2 co-design sessions with 6 consumers; about 6 weeks. Standard: one organisation or major program; 6 interviews, 3 sessions with 8 consumers; about 8 weeks. Full: a whole organisation or a multi-site program; 10 interviews, 6 sessions with two groups of 8 consumers; about 12 weeks.

## How it runs

Engagement is designed from the decision backwards: starting with what the organisation needs to know, and building the approach that gets there credibly.

1. **Discovery.** I establish the decisions the engagement has to inform, what is already known, and who needs to be in the room.
2. **Co-design.** I run sessions with consumers, who are paid at published rates for their time.
3. **Synthesis.** I bring what was heard into a form your decision-makers can use, and test it with the people who contributed.
4. **Framework and handover.** You receive a framework your organisation can run, and I walk your team through it.

## Why it matters

Poor engagement creates risk. Strong engagement creates confidence, internally, externally, and in the decisions that follow.
