# Business brain: Kris Pierce Consulting, engagement advisory

Line 1 of 3. Status: sellable now, once prices and the conflict wording are decided. Brand: Kris Pierce Consulting v3.0 (locked). Site: krispierce.com.au only.

## 1. The business in one paragraph

Organisations that make decisions about rare disease research, evidence and access are now expected to show how consumers shaped those decisions, and most cannot. Kris Pierce Consulting tells them where their engagement stands, fixes the part that matters most, and stays on as an adviser where that is useful. The front door is a free diagnostic. Everything after it has a fixed scope and a published starting price.

## 2. Why now

- The Framework for consumer engagement in HTA was published on 7 July 2026, and the PBAC Guidelines are being updated. Sponsors and patient organisations now have a standard to meet.
- The Strategic Agreement between the Commonwealth and Medicines Australia ends in 2027, so engagement commitments are being renegotiated.
- IQVIA reported that 66% of Australian drug HTA submissions in 2024 included consumer input and that the evidence was underused. Large firms are moving in; none has lived and committee-side experience.
- No commercially priced consumer engagement maturity tool was found in Australia. The free ones (Safety and Quality Commission, Safer Care Victoria) are written for hospitals. The CEOF Diagnostic has the field to itself for research, industry and patient organisations.

Sources in `../research/01-ai-and-consulting-market.md` and `../research/03-licensing-navigation-diagnostic-podcast.md`.

## 5. Strategy

**Position.** The independent specialist in consumer engagement for rare disease evidence and access, who has done the work as a carer, as a founder, as a programme lead and from inside the advisory system. Not a facilitator and not an agency. The existing line holds: "If the work doesn't change a decision, it isn't done."

**How it wins.** Large firms sell teams and methodology. Kris sells judgement, speed and a fixed scope. The diagnostic gives prospects a result before they speak to anyone, which is rare in this market.

**Moat.** The CEOF dataset. Every completed diagnostic adds to a benchmark no one else has: where Australian organisations stand, by sector. From about 50 completions, publish an annual "State of consumer engagement" note. It is the content engine and the reason to take the diagnostic.

**Capacity rule.** No more than two retainers and one project at a time. When that is full, raise prices before adding days.

**Twelve-month targets (base case).** 60 diagnostics completed, 8 debriefs, 3 sprints, 2 retainers, 2 training days: about $137,000 from about 55 days.
