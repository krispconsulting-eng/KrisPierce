## Day 3: one thing to do with the results

**Subject:** One question to ask of your results

Hi [First name],

A report like this is easy to file. Here is one way to use it this week.

Take your seven domain results to the person who owns your next big decision: a grant application, a program design, a partnership. Ask one question: "Which of these results would an outsider notice first?"

The answer is usually your first priority. It is rarely the lowest number. It is the one that sits closest to a decision other people are watching.

If you reply and tell me what the answer was, I will tell you what I would look at next. No charge and no pitch.

Best regards,
Kris
