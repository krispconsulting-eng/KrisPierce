# Kris Pierce Consulting

**Independent advice on consumer engagement for rare disease research, evidence and access.**

## What I do

You are now expected to show how consumers shaped your decisions about rare disease research, evidence and access. I tell you where your engagement stands, fix the part that matters most, and stay on as an adviser where that is useful.
