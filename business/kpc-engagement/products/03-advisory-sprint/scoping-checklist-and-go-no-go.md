## 7. Go or no-go rule

Count the **M** lines that are not ticked.

| Result | Decision | What Kris does |
|---|---|---|
| Every **M** line ticked | **Go** | Send the one-page quote within two working days. On a yes, write the proposal from `../../assets/proposal-template.md` and record the conflict check in the tracker. |
| One or two **M** lines unticked in sections 1, 2, 3, 5 or 6, and the client can fix them within a week | **Hold** | Tell the client exactly what is missing and by when. Do not quote. If fixed, re-run this checklist. If not fixed in a week, it becomes a no-go. |
| Any **M** line unticked in section 2 that cannot be fixed | **No-go: too big** | Offer a scoping call for Consumer Engagement Design, or a debrief first. |
| No decision owner or no date after asking twice | **No-go: no decision owner** | Say a sprint needs someone to decide. Offer the debrief, or leave it. |
| Any **M** line unticked in section 4 | **No-go: decline or reshape** | Reshape the question until it is clearly separate and re-run from section 1, or decline in writing. Never quote with a section 4 line open. |
| Capacity line unticked | **Go, later** | Offer the next free start date. Do not squeeze it in. When full, raise prices before adding days. |

Non-**M** lines unticked do not stop a quote. List them in the quote under "what I need from you" or as assumptions.
