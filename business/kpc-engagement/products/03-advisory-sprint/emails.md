# Strategic Advisory Sprint: emails

> **What this is:** the five emails that carry a sprint from kickoff to day 60. Fill the [square brackets]. Sent from info@krispierce.com.au. Nothing is sent without Kris's approval.
> The proposal and acceptance come first and are in `../../assets/proposal-template.md`.

## 2. Interview invitation (week 1)

> Sent by the client's contact, so the invitation comes from someone the interviewee knows. Kris supplies this text. Two versions.

### 2a. Staff, board members and partners

**Subject:** 45 minutes with Kris Pierce on [short name of the decision]

Hi [first name],

[Organisation] is deciding [decision, in plain words], and has asked Kris Pierce of Kris Pierce Consulting to advise. Kris is speaking with a small number of people before writing a paper for [decision owner or leadership team], and I would like you to be one of them.

What is involved:

- One conversation of 45 minutes, by video, between [date] and [date].
- No preparation. Kris will ask how you see the situation and what you would do.
- What you say is reported without your name. Kris will not attribute a view to you unless you agree.

To choose a time: [booking link, or "reply with two times that suit"].

Thank you,
[client contact]

### 2b. Consumers, carers and community members

**Subject:** An invitation to share your view, paid, 45 minutes

Hi [first name],

[Organisation] is deciding [decision, in plain words]. Before that decision is made, the organisation wants to hear from people it affects. I am writing to ask whether you would be willing to speak with Kris Pierce, an independent adviser who is helping with the decision.

What is involved:

- One conversation of 45 minutes, between [date] and [date], by video or phone, whichever you prefer.
- You will be paid [amount] for your time, by [payer], within [number] days. [Any expenses covered.]
- You are welcome to see the questions beforehand and to have a support person with you.
- You speak only for yourself and your own experience. You can skip any question or stop at any time.
- What you say is reported without your name.
- Afterwards you will be told what was decided and how what you said was used.

Taking part is your choice, and saying no will not affect your relationship with [organisation] in any way.

If you would like to take part, [booking link, or "reply to this email and I will arrange a time"]. If you have questions first, you can reach me on [contact].

Thank you,
[client contact]

## 5. 60-day follow-up (day 60 after the briefing)

**Subject:** 60 days on from the decision

Hi [first name],

It is 60 days since your leadership team decided to [decision as made]. I said I would ask how it has gone.

Three questions. Short answers are plenty.

1. Of the first-30-days actions, what got done, and what did not?
2. What has turned out to be harder than the paper suggested?
3. Has your community seen anything change yet?

If something has stalled, tell me where. I will reply with what I would try, at no charge. That is part of the sprint.

[Use one paragraph below, or none. Choose by what the client has said since handover, not by what would be good to sell.]

[If the work is going well and the client is carrying it:] If it is all in hand, that is the best result, and you do not need anything further from me. I would be glad to hear what has changed.

[If the client has been asking questions since handover:] You have had a few questions for me since the handover, and I have been glad to answer them. If that kind of advice would be useful on a regular footing, I work with a small number of organisations as a retained adviser: a monthly working session, document review and advice on call, from $3,500 a month over six months. If it would help, I can send a one-page outline. If not, keep asking when something comes up.

[If the recommendation included a build that has not started:] The paper recommended [build]. If finding the time to design it is what is holding it up, I can scope it as a fixed-price project. I only take one project at a time, so tell me if you would like me to hold a place.

One last thing: with your written consent, I would like to be able to name [organisation] as a client. If you would prefer I did not, I will not, and you do not need to give a reason.

Best regards,
Kris
