# Strategic Advisory Sprint: close-out checklist

Internal. Run it within two working days of the leadership briefing, inside the 1.5 hours of handover time in week 4 (`sprint-plan.md`), and return to the last section at day 60. The handover email and the 60-day email are in `emails.md` (4 and 5) and are not repeated here. This file holds the checks around them, plus two pieces of wording that exist nowhere else: the consent to be named and the testimonial request.

## 5. Permission to name the client

Never name a client, or describe the work in public, without written consent. Ask once, in writing, after the final invoice is paid, or in the 60-day email, which already carries a one-line version. Ask before asking for a testimonial, and never in the same sentence as an offer of more work.

Wording:

> Hi [first name],
>
> A request, and no is a perfectly good answer.
>
> With your written consent, I would like to be able to say that [organisation] has been a client of Kris Pierce Consulting. You can choose how far that goes:
>
> 1. Name only: "[organisation]" in a list of clients.
> 2. Name and one line: "[organisation]: [sprint type], [year]". I would send you the exact line first.
> 3. Not at all.
>
> Whichever you choose, I will not publish your results, your decision paper or anything said in interviews. You can withdraw consent at any time by email and I will remove the mention within five working days. If you would prefer I did not name you, I will not, and you do not need to give a reason.
>
> [Company clients only:] Please check this against your own disclosure and communications policies before replying. Nothing I publish will refer to any product.
>
> Best regards,
> Kris

- [ ] Asked on: [date]. Answer: [option 1 / option 2 / not at all / no reply].
- [ ] If yes: the reply is saved with the client file, and the exact wording approved is: [wording].
- [ ] If no, or no reply: recorded, and not asked again.

## 6. Testimonial request

Only real words from a real client, used exactly as approved. Never drafted for them, never edited beyond what they approve, never invented, never paid for. Ask only if the client has said the work was useful, and only after consent to be named or with a clear option to stay unnamed. Do not ask a company for a testimonial unless its compliance team is likely to allow one; ask the contact first whether it is possible.

Wording:

> Hi [first name],
>
> You mentioned that [what they said, in their words]. If you are willing, I would value two or three sentences from you that I could show to organisations deciding whether to work with me.
>
> Three questions that may help, and you can ignore them:
>
> 1. What was the decision, and where were you with it before the sprint?
> 2. What changed?
> 3. What would you tell someone in your position who was considering it?
>
> Please write it in your own words. I will not edit it, apart from shortening with your agreement, and I will send you the final version to approve before it appears anywhere. Tell me how you would like to be credited: your name, role and organisation; your role and the type of organisation only; or without any attribution. You can withdraw it at any time.
>
> If you would rather not, that is completely fine.
>
> Best regards,
> Kris
