# Strategic Advisory Sprint: decision paper template

> **What this is:** the sprint deliverable as a fill-in template. Eight pages at most, one section per page or less. Fill every [square bracket], delete the internal notes (quoted blocks), export to PDF.
> **Rules:** the recommendation appears on page 1. Findings are sourced. Interviewees are not named without agreement. Consumer payments are a separate line. Run the quality bar in `PRODUCT.md` section 8 before sending.
> **Voice:** Kris writes as "I". The second heading is "What I found", never "we".
> **Footer on every page:** Prepared by Kris Pierce Consulting for [organisation]. Confidential. Not medical advice.
