# Strategic Advisory Sprint: one-page quote template

> **What this is:** a one-page fixed-price quote, sent within two working days of a "go" on `scoping-checklist-and-go-no-go.md`. It lets the client say yes or no to the decision, the price and the dates before the full proposal is written.
> **What it is not:** the contract. Terms (scope detail, payment terms, conflict check, Code clause, intellectual property, confidentiality, acceptance) are in the full proposal, written from `../../assets/proposal-template.md` once the client says yes. Do not copy those terms here.
> **Rules:** one page after export. Fill every [square bracket], delete this block and any line marked "delete if not applicable". Nothing is sent without Kris's approval.
