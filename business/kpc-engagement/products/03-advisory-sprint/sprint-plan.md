# Strategic Advisory Sprint: sprint plan

Internal. Week-by-week plan with hours. A day is 8 hours; the sprint is 5 days, 40 hours. Hours are caps, not targets. The client-facing version of this timeline goes in section 5 of the proposal (`../../assets/proposal-template.md`).

## Before the sprint starts (pre-sale, not counted in the 5 days)

- [ ] Proposal accepted in writing, with the decision, the decision owner and the decision date.
- [ ] Sprint type named: consumer evidence plan, engagement strategy, trial-readiness review or partnership approach.
- [ ] Conflict check done and recorded (proposal section 9).
- [ ] First invoice paid.
- [ ] Kickoff and leadership briefing both booked. The briefing date is fixed before work starts.
- [ ] Kickoff email sent (`emails.md`, 1).

## Week 1: kickoff, document review, interviews booked (1.0 day, 8 hours)

| Task | Hours | Output | File |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prepare the kickoff: re-read the proposal and any debrief action plan; draft the interview list | 1.0 | Kickoff notes | `kickoff-agenda.md` |
| Kickoff with the decision owner and contact | 1.0 | Decision confirmed; interviewees named; documents requested | `kickoff-agenda.md` |
| Tailor the interview guide to the sprint type; send the invitation text to the client's contact | 1.0 | Guide ready; invitations out | `interview-guide.md`, `emails.md` (2) |
| Document review: up to 15 documents or about 150 pages | 5.0 | A one-page evidence log: what each document says about the decision; gaps; questions for interviews | |
| **Week 1 total** | **8.0** | | |

From the client this week: documents within five working days; interviews booked; consumer payment arrangements confirmed if consumers are being interviewed.

## Week 2: interviews, first-pass analysis, mid-point update (1.25 days, 10 hours)

| Task | Hours | Output | File |
|---|---|---|---|
| Up to six interviews, 45 minutes each, plus 15 minutes of notes straight after | 6.0 | Notes per interview, in the note format in the guide | `interview-guide.md` |
| First-pass analysis: sort what was heard against the decision; list where interviews and documents agree, disagree and are silent | 3.0 | Five to eight findings in draft; two or three candidate options | |
| Mid-point update, written, to the decision owner | 1.0 | One-page update; any change of scope raised now | `emails.md` (3) |
| **Week 2 total** | **10.0** | | |

From the client this week: interviewees turn up; a reply to the mid-point update within two working days.

## Week 3: analysis, options, draft paper (1.75 days, 14 hours)

| Task | Hours | Output | File |
|---|---|---|---|
| Finish the analysis: test each finding against its sources; drop what cannot be supported | 2.0 | Final findings | |
| Build the options: two or three plus "do nothing"; cost, time, people and trade-offs for each | 3.0 | Options table | |
| Write the draft decision paper | 8.0 | Draft, eight pages or fewer | `decision-paper-template.md` |
| Call the decision owner (20 minutes) to state the recommendation before the draft lands; send the draft | 1.0 | Draft with the client; comments due in three working days | |
| **Week 3 total** | **14.0** | | |

From the client this week: one round of consolidated comments within three working days.

## Week 4: final paper, leadership briefing, handover (1.0 day, 8 hours)

| Task | Hours | Output | File |

## If time runs short

Cut in this order, and tell the client at the mid-point update:

1. Interviews: six becomes five or four. Keep the decision owner and at least one community voice.
2. Paper length: eight pages becomes six. Keep the decision, recommendation, what it takes and first 30 days.
3. Slides: ten becomes seven (drop slides 3, 8 and 9 in the outline).

Never cut: the call to the decision owner before the draft, the briefing, or the out-of-scope section.
