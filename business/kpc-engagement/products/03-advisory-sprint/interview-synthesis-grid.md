**How to rate evidence strength**

| Rating | Test | How it appears in the paper |
|---|---|---|
| Strong | A document supports it and at least two interviews do; or four or more interviews across at least two groups | Stated as a finding |
| Moderate | At least two interviews, or one document, and nobody contradicts it | Stated as a finding, with the source count |
| Single source | One interview only, no document | Only if it bears on the decision, and marked "raised by one interviewee" |
| Contested | Two or more interviews on each side | Under "where people disagree", both positions stated fairly, without names |

A pattern with no implication for the decision does not go in the paper, however interesting. Move it to "questions this work raised" in section 8 of the paper, or drop it.

## Step 4. Checks before writing

- [ ] Every finding marked "yes" passes the rule in the interview guide: a document supports it, or at least two interviews do.
- [ ] What consumers, carers and community members said is visible as its own set of points, not averaged into staff views. Their numbers: [numbers].
- [ ] Where consumers and staff see it differently, that difference is itself a finding or is stated under "where people disagree".
- [ ] The decision owner's view has not been counted twice or weighted above the others.
- [ ] No more than eight findings. If there are more, merge or cut the ones with the weakest implication.
- [ ] No finding could identify a person, by role in a small team or by a detail of their story.
- [ ] Any quotation has the speaker's agreement recorded in step 1.
- [ ] Gaps are listed: [what could not be established, and whether it matters to the decision].
- [ ] Nothing in the grid concerns a specific application to a committee. If something was said, it is deleted from the notes and not used.
