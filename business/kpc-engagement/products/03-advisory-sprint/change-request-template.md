# Strategic Advisory Sprint: change request template

> **What this is:** what Kris uses when a client asks for more, or for something different, part way through a sprint. Part A is the internal test. Part B is the note sent to the client. Part C is the log.
> **Why it exists:** the proposal says a change to the decision or the scope "is agreed in writing and may change the fee and dates". This is that writing. The five days are a cap, so nothing extra is absorbed quietly.
> **When to raise it:** as soon as the request is made, and at the mid-point update at the latest. Never in the decision paper.

---

## Part A. Internal test (five minutes)

The request, in the client's words: [client: request]. Asked by: [client: name]. Date: [date]. Sprint week: [1 / 2 / 3 / 4].

| # | Question | Answer | If yes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Is it product-specific, or does it touch a submission or a committee? | [yes / no] | Decline it. Use response 4. Re-run the conflict check if circumstances have changed |
| 2 | Does it change the decision the sprint serves? | [yes / no] | Stop and agree new wording with the decision owner. Use response 2 or 3 |
| 3 | Is it already inside the scope, and only feels new? | [yes / no] | Do it. No change request. Note it in the log |
| 4 | Can it be swapped for something of the same size (one interviewee for another, one document for another)? | [yes / no] | Use response 1 |
| 5 | Does it add hours without changing the type of work (a seventh interview, a second round of comments, a second briefing)? | [yes / no] | Use response 2 |
| 6 | Is it a different kind of work (workshops, a survey, sessions with consumers, carrying out the recommendation, ongoing advice)? | [yes / no] | Use response 3 |

Hours it would add: [Kris: hours]. Effect on the briefing date: [none / moves to (date)]. Consumer payments affected: [yes, $(amount) / no].

## Part B. Note to the client

**Subject:** Your request on [short name]: what it changes

Hi [first name],

You asked whether I could [request, in one line]. Here is what that means for the sprint.

[Use one response, delete the others.]

**Response 1. A swap, no change to fee or dates.**
I can do that in place of [what it replaces]. The fee and the dates stay as they are. The trade-off is [what is lost by the swap]. If you are happy with that, reply "agreed" and I will go ahead.

**Response 2. An addition, with a fee and a date.**
That sits outside the scope you and I agreed, so I would treat it as an addition:

| | |
|---|---|
| What is added | [one line] |
| Additional fee | $[amount], excluding GST, fixed [Kris: confirm the basis for additions; the day-rate basis in the business plan is $2,400] |
| Consumer payments, separate | [$ amount at (published rate schedule), or "none"] |
| Effect on dates | [draft paper moves to (date); briefing moves to (date); or "none"] |
| What stays the same | The decision, the eight-page limit and everything else in the proposal |

If you want it, reply "agreed" by [date]. After that date I will carry on with the scope as it stands, so the briefing date holds. If you would prefer to leave it, I will list it in the paper under "what was out of scope" so it is not lost.

**Response 3. Not part of a sprint.**
That is a different piece of work from the one decision this sprint serves, and trying to fit it in would weaken both. I suggest finishing the sprint as agreed. I will name it in the paper under "what was out of scope", and if you want it afterwards I can scope it as [a Consumer Engagement Design project / a retained advisory arrangement / a training day / a second sprint on that decision], with its own fixed price.

[If the decision itself has changed:] The decision in the proposal was "[original wording]". What you are now describing is "[new wording]". I need you and [decision owner] to confirm which one I am working to, in writing, by [date]. A change at this point moves the dates to [dates] and [changes / does not change] the fee.

**Response 4. Outside what I do.**
I have to leave that outside this work. I do not work on product-specific submissions or advise on how a committee is likely to respond. Everything about your general engagement capability stays in scope, and the sprint carries on as agreed.

Best regards,
Kris

Rules:

- Nothing changes until the client has replied in writing. A yes on a call is followed by this note.
- An agreed addition is invoiced with the final invoice, as its own line.
- Two additions in one sprint is a sign the work was a project, not a sprint. Say so at the mid-point update.
- A change to consumer interviews is not confirmed until payment for the extra people is arranged.
- If a conflict has appeared, section 9 of the proposal applies: change the scope or stop, and the client pays only for work done.
