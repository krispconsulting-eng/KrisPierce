# In-house co-design training: participant evaluation forms

> **What this is:** two short forms. The end-of-day form (8 questions) is handed out at 4:20 and collected before people leave. The 30-day form (5 questions) goes out with the reminder for the follow-up call and is collected before or on the call. Fill in the [square brackets] and delete this note.
> **How the answers are used:** to improve the day, and in the close-out to the sponsor in general terms with no individual singled out. Answers are anonymous unless a participant adds their name. Nothing is quoted publicly without that person's written consent. No answer is treated as a testimonial unless the person is asked separately and agrees.

---

> **INTERNAL: what I do with the forms**
>
> | Form | When | What I do |
> |---|---|---|
> | End of day | The same evening | Read all. Note anyone who answered "no" to question 1 and offer a 15-minute call to finish the plan. Add barriers from question 7 to the "needs" table in the summary note (`follow-up.md`, section 1). |
> | End of day | Within three working days | Tally question 2 by learning outcome. Any outcome where half the group is at 1 or 2 is covered again on the 30-day call. |
> | 30 days | Day 28 | Read before the call. Use question 1 for the status round and question 4 to choose the common obstacle (`follow-up.md`, section 3). |
> | Both | After the call | Record totals only in the close-out to the sponsor: how many run, booked, changed, stalled. No individual is singled out. |
> | Both | After close-out | Note what to change in the run sheet. Delete named forms after [period; Kris: confirm]. |
>
> - [ ] The eight end-of-day questions and five follow-up questions are unchanged between clients, so results can be compared over time.
> - [ ] No result is published, and no figure is quoted in marketing, without Kris's approval and the client's written consent.
