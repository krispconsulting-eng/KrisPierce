# In-house co-design training: facilitator checklist

> **What this is:** my own checklist for one training booking, in four parts: the week before, the day before, on the day, the week after. It follows the delivery steps in `PRODUCT.md`, section 7, and the timings in `run-sheet.md`; it adds nothing to the scope. Tick and date each line. Internal.

**Client:** [organisation]. **Date of the day:** [date]. **Format:** [in person / online, two half days / online, one day]. **Sponsor:** [name]. **Organiser:** [name]. **30-day call:** [date, time].

## Already done before this checklist starts

- [ ] Scoping call held; conflict check on the projects recorded.
- [ ] Proposal accepted; first invoice paid.
- [ ] Booking form returned (`booking-form.md`).
- [ ] Run sheet and examples tailored to the sector; workbook sent for printing; room and materials confirmed (two weeks before).

## The week before

- [ ] Seven days before: pre-work email sent to every participant, by me or the organiser as agreed (`pre-work.md`). Date: [date].
- [ ] Three days before: reminder sent only to those who have not replied.
- [ ] Workbooks printed, A4, single-sided: one per participant plus two spare. Front matter filled in: organisation, date, 30-day call date. [Kris: confirm whether printing sits inside the fee.]
- [ ] Published rate schedule named in workbook section 4.1, and checked against the primary source. [Kris: schedule to cite.]
- [ ] Support contacts in workbook section 5.1 confirmed current. [Kris: contacts to list.]
- [ ] Slides checked against `slide-outline.md`; sector examples swapped in; no client names, no invented cases.
- [ ] Materials packed against `PRODUCT.md`, section 9: five A3 spectrum cards; 3 sets of 12 activity cards; 4 sets of 4 scenario cards; 12 markers; 6 pads of sticky notes in three colours; sticky dots; 15 name labels; tape; timer; "Parked questions" sheet; 15 pens.
- [ ] Organiser confirmed: room layout, flip chart stands, screen, quiet space, catering times, who lets me in at 8:15.
- [ ] Access needs from the booking form built into the day: [text].
- [ ] Travel and accommodation booked, at cost, as agreed in writing.
- [ ] Co-presenter or panel, if booked: briefing call held; sessions and introduction agreed in writing; payment line approved; payment details collected. Date: [date].
- [ ] Online: whiteboard set up with the spectrum and the cards pre-loaded; breakout rooms planned in threes; fillable workbook sent five days ahead; co-host briefed. [Kris: whiteboard tool.]

## The day before

*`PRODUCT.md` puts the reading of pre-work two days before, 1.5 hours. Do it then; this is the last check.*

- [ ] Every pre-work response read. "Our projects" list written.
- [ ] Every participant has a project, or is paired. No more than six projects.
- [ ] Conflict check on answers 1 to 3: no project tied to an application before a committee I sit on or advise. Any that is has been swapped. Date: [date].
- [ ] Table groups drawn up: people sharing a project together; otherwise roles and seniority mixed.
- [ ] Answer 6 collated, unattributed, to read out at 9:18 as "what this group wants from today".
- [ ] Who has a clear decision and who does not, noted for session 2.
- [ ] Two or three patterns across the projects noted for 9:18.
- [ ] Someone attending holds a budget. Name: [name].
- [ ] Sponsor confirmed for 9:00 and 4:00, and briefed on what the "needs" list at 4:05 is likely to hold.
- [ ] Day outline flip chart written. Run sheet printed.
- [ ] Laptop charged; HDMI adapter; slides on the laptop and on a stick.
- [ ] Route and arrival time checked for 8:15.

## On the day

**Before 9:00**

- [ ] 8:15 arrive; tables in groups of three or four.
- [ ] 8:25 spectrum cards on the wall, left to right; parked sheet up; outline up.
- [ ] 8:35 workbooks, pens, sticky notes, dots and name labels on tables; activity and scenario cards ready, face down.
- [ ] 8:45 screen and timer tested; quiet space checked; greet people and ask each for their project in one sentence.

**During**

- [ ] 9:06 ground rules agreed aloud, including that no one has to share anything personal and anyone can step out.
- [ ] Each project checked quietly against the conflict rule; anyone affected spoken to at the first break.
- [ ] My input kept to 15 minutes or less in every session.
- [ ] No dollar rate quoted from memory in session 3; the named schedule or the organisation's policy only.
- [ ] 11:45 payment totals said aloud and written on the flip chart.
- [ ] Before the practice at 1:05: said that roles may be played lightly and anyone may sit out. Room watched; quiet space used if needed.
- [ ] 1:50 to 2:15: the 25 minutes on the session plan protected. Every plan read and checked for four things: the decision, the level, a payment line, a loop date.
- [ ] Afternoon tea: sponsor briefed on what is coming at 4:05.
- [ ] 4:15 sponsor responded to the "needs" flip chart with who and by when.
- [ ] 4:20 the 30-day call date written on the last page of every workbook; evaluation forms handed out and collected.
- [ ] Finished at 4:30.

**If time ran short** (from the run sheet): eight cards not 12; three scenarios not four; two rows of the table. Never cut: the 25 minutes on the session plan, the payment totals, the sponsor's response.

**After 4:30**

- [ ] Wall spectrum, "Our projects", totals, phrases and needs photographed.
- [ ] Notes for the summary note written while fresh. Parked questions copied.
- [ ] Packed down by 4:45. Room left as found.
- [ ] Anyone who seemed affected during the day contacted that evening or the next morning.

## The week after

- [ ] Evaluation forms read the same evening; anyone without a finished plan offered a 15-minute call.
- [ ] Within three working days: summary note sent to all participants and the sponsor (`follow-up.md`, section 1), with projects, commitments, the needs table and the sponsor's responses, the participation cost total, and answers to parked questions. Date: [date].
- [ ] Workbook file attached to the summary note.
- [ ] Certificates of completion issued, if the client asked for them (`certificate-of-completion-template.md`). Date: [date].
- [ ] Co-presenter or panel paid within 14 days, with expenses; thanked in writing. Date: [date].
- [ ] Expenses invoiced at cost, as agreed in writing. Final invoice issued per the proposal terms. Date: [date].
- [ ] Access and dietary information deleted. Photographs kept only as needed for the summary note.
- [ ] Calendar entries set: day 25, reminder and 30-day form (`follow-up.md`, section 2; `participant-evaluation-form.md`, form 2); day 28, re-read commitments; day 30, the call; within two working days after, close-out emails.
- [ ] Notes on what to change in the run sheet next time: [Kris: add after each day].

## After the 30-day call (for completeness)

- [ ] Status recorded per project: run, booked, changed, stalled.
- [ ] Close-out email to participants and to the sponsor (`follow-up.md`, section 4). A next step is offered only if the call showed the need.
- [ ] Written consent requested to name the organisation. No name is used without it.
- [ ] Pipeline tracker updated.
