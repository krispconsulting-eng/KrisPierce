# In-house co-design training: pre-work

> **What this is:** the email sent to participants seven days before the training day, with one short task, and Kris's notes on how the responses are used. Sent by the client's organiser or by Kris from info@krispierce.com.au, as agreed at scoping. Fill the [square brackets].

---

## Pre-work email (seven days before)

**Subject:** Co-design training on [date]: one 20-minute task before the day

Hi [first name],

On [day, date] you are joining a day of practical training on involving community members in decisions, from 9:00 to 4:30 at [venue]. I am running it.

The day is built around your own work. You will spend it planning a real session with community members for a real project, and you will leave with a one-page plan you can use. That only works if you arrive with a project in mind, so there is one task to do first. It takes about 20 minutes.

**The task: choose one project and answer six questions.**

Choose a project that is coming up in the next six months where patients, carers, families or community members are affected, and where they are involved or should be. Small is fine. Real is essential. If you share a project with a colleague who is also attending, you can both use it.

Please do not choose a project connected to a specific application to a health technology or funding committee. If that is the only project you have, reply and I will help you find another.

1. What is the project, in one sentence?
2. Who does it affect?
3. What decision has to be made, who makes it, and by when?
4. What have you planned so far to involve the people it affects, if anything?
5. Think of one time you involved community members, or were involved as one. What went well, or what would you do differently? Two or three sentences.
6. What is the one thing you most want to be able to do after this day?

Reply to this email with your answers by [date, three days before]. Dot points are fine. I read every response and use them to shape the day. I will not share your answers with your manager.

**On the day**

- Arrive from 8:45 for a 9:00 start. Morning tea, lunch and afternoon tea are provided.
- Bring a pen and anything written about your project: a brief, a timeline, a budget. No laptop is needed.
- You will receive a printed workbook.
- If you have access needs I should know about, reply and tell me or tell [organiser].

One more thing. Some of the day touches on difficult experiences, and some of you will have lived them as patients or carers as well as at work. You will never be asked to share anything personal, and you can step out at any time.

Best regards,
Kris

Kris Pierce Consulting
info@krispierce.com.au

> **Internal: how Kris uses the responses (1.5 hours, two days before)**
>
> | Answer | Use |
> |---|---|
> | 1, 2 | "Our projects" list; table groups (people sharing a project sit together; otherwise mix roles and seniority) |
> | 3 | Who has a clear decision and who does not. Those who do not get attention in session 2 |
> | 1 to 3 | Conflict check: any project tied to an application before a committee Kris sits on or advises is swapped before the day |
> | 4 | Where the room sits on the spectrum now; which sector examples to use |
> | 5 | Themes for sessions 4 and 7. Never quoted in the room without the person's permission |
> | 6 | Read out, unattributed, at 9:18 as "what this group wants from today" |
>
> - [ ] Every participant has a project, or is paired.
> - [ ] No more than six projects in the room.
> - [ ] Someone attending holds a budget.
> - [ ] Sponsor confirmed for 9:00 and 4:00.
> - [ ] Access needs passed to the organiser.
> - [ ] Workbooks printed: participants plus two.
