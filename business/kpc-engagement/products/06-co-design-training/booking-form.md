# In-house co-design training: booking form

> **What this is:** the form the client's organiser completes when booking the training day. It gives me what I need to tailor the day, group the tables, run the conflict check on the projects and meet access needs. Send it with the quote, or straight after acceptance. Return date: at least three weeks before the day. Fill in the [square brackets] and delete this note.
> **Privacy:** ask only for what is here. Access and dietary needs are used to run the day and deleted afterwards. No one is asked about their own health or caring role.
