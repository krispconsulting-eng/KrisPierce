# In-house co-design training: certificate of completion template

> **What this is:** the wording for a simple certificate, issued only where the client asks for one. It records attendance and the learning outcomes the day covered. It makes no accreditation claim: the training is not an accredited course, carries no assessment and gives no qualification (`PRODUCT.md`, section 5).
> **Who gets one:** a participant who attended the whole day, or both half days online. Someone who missed more than one session receives the attendance note at the end of this file instead.
> **Design:** one A4 page, landscape or portrait, in the Kris Pierce Consulting system only: the script wordmark, Outfit for the name and title, Archivo for everything else, on cream. No seals, crests, ribbons, borders that imitate a qualification, or other organisations' logos. The client's logo is not added. Fill in the [square brackets].

## Certificate wording

**Kris Pierce Consulting**

# Certificate of completion

This records that

## [participant: full name]

of [client: organisation]

attended **Co-design in practice**, a one-day in-house training in involving community members in decisions, on [date] [at venue, city / online on date and date].

**Duration:** [one day, 9:00 to 4:30, including breaks / two half days, 9:30 to 1:00 each, including breaks].

**What the day covered.** Each participant worked on a real project of their own and practised how to:

1. explain what co-design is and is not, and place an activity on the IAP2 Spectrum of Public Participation: inform, consult, involve, collaborate, empower;
2. name the decision a project needs, and choose the level of involvement that decision warrants;
3. write a recruitment plan that reaches beyond the people already known to the organisation;
4. cost participation properly: payment at published rates, expenses and access, as a separate budget line;
5. plan and run a session that is safe: informed consent, the right to withdraw, a plan for distress, care with stories;
6. turn what people said into options and a decision, and show which input changed what;
7. close the loop: tell participants what happened to their input, including what was not taken up and why;
8. complete a one-page session plan for their own project.

Kris Pierce

## Wording I do not use

- "Certified", "accredited", "qualified", "licensed" or "competent in".
- "Certified co-design facilitator" or any title a participant could put after their name.
- Continuing professional development points or hours "awarded". If a participant's professional body lets members self-record learning hours, the duration line above gives them the facts; I make no claim about how any body will treat it.
- Any reference to my other roles or appointments.
- "On behalf of" or "in partnership with" any other organisation.
