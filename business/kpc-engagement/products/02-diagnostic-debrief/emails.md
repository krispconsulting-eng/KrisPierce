# Diagnostic Debrief: emails

> **What this is:** the four emails that carry a debrief from booking to day 30. Fill the [square brackets]. Sent from info@krispierce.com.au. Nothing is sent without Kris's approval.
> The short confirmation message shown by the booking tool is in `../../assets/diagnostic-debrief-offer.md` (c). Email 1 below is the fuller email that follows it.

## 1. Booking confirmation (day of booking)

**Subject:** Your Diagnostic Debrief on [date]: one short task

Hi [first name],

Thank you for booking a Diagnostic Debrief. The details:

- When: [day, date, time and time zone], 90 minutes
- Where: [video link]
- Who: you and up to three colleagues

By the end of the session you and I will have agreed three priorities, what to stop, and who owns what over the next 90 days. Within five working days you will have it in writing, in a plan of up to six pages.

One task before then. Please answer eight short questions here: [questionnaire link]. It takes about ten minutes, and I would like it back by [date, two working days before]. I will read your diagnostic results and your answers before the session, so there is nothing else to prepare.

Two things make the session work:

1. Bring at least one person who can agree to owners and dates on the day.
2. If the decision or deadline behind this booking changes, reply and tell me.

The invoice for $1,500 plus GST is attached. If you book a Strategic Advisory Sprint within 60 days of the session, the fee is credited against it.

Best regards,
Kris

**Subject:** 30 days in: how are the first actions going?

Hi [first name],

It is 30 days since the debrief. Your plan had [number] actions due in days 1 to 30:

- [action 1, owner]
- [action 2, owner]
- [action 3, owner]

Three questions, and a one-line answer to each is plenty:

1. Which of these are done?
2. Which one is stuck, and on what?
3. Did you stop [activity agreed to stop]?

If something is stuck, tell me what is in the way and I will reply with what I would try next.

[Only if page 6 of the plan named a sprint decision:] One practical note. The plan named [decision] as the one worth outside help. If you decide you want that, the debrief fee is credited against a sprint booked by [date]. There is no need to reply on that point unless it is useful.

Best regards,
Kris
