## Part 5. Decision

Rule: every line in part 3 must be "clear" to accept as booked. One "not clear" means reshape or decline. Part 2 lines can be fixed before the session; part 3 lines cannot be fixed by hoping.

| | |
|---|---|
| [ ] Accept | All of part 3 clear; part 2 lines 1 and 6 yes. Send confirmation, questionnaire and invoice (`emails.md`, 1). |
| [ ] Accept with a changed scope | Something in part 3 was not clear and has been separated out in writing. What was excluded: [Kris: one sentence]. Client agreed on: [date]. |
| [ ] Hold | Waiting on: [Kris: review completion, a decision-maker, a declaration]. Chase once on: [date]. |
| [ ] Decline | Reason, in one sentence: [Kris: reason]. Reply sent on: [date]. Any fee already paid refunded on: [date]. |

Wording if declining for a conflict: "Thank you for booking. I have to decline, because the work would sit too close to a matter before a committee I sit on or advise, and I keep those apart. I am sorry not to be able to help on this occasion."

Wording if reshaping: "Before I confirm, one boundary. I can work with you on how your organisation engages, as a capability. I cannot work on [excluded matter]. If the debrief is still useful on that basis, I will confirm the booking."
