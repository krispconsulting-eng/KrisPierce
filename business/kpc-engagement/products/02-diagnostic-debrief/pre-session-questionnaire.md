**Before your Diagnostic Debrief**

Eight questions, about ten minutes. Short answers are fine; dot points are fine. I read every answer before the session, so the 90 minutes go on decisions and not on background.

1. **What decision or deadline is behind this booking?** Name it and give the date if there is one. (For example: a grant application due in March, a board paper, a funder report, a new program starting.)

2. **In one or two sentences, what does your organisation do, and who is the community you involve or want to involve?**

3. **Who completed the CEOF Diagnostic?** One person, or a group? Was any score argued over?

4. **Which result surprised you most, and why?**

5. **Which result do you think is wrong, too high or too low?** Tell me what I would see if I looked.

6. **What engagement activity are you running now?** List up to five things: groups, surveys, events, paid roles, anything. Put a star next to any you suspect is not worth the effort.

7. **What do you have to work with over the next 90 days?** Roughly: staff time, budget (including any budget to pay community members), and who can approve spending.

8. **If this debrief goes well, what will be different 90 days from now?**

**Who is joining the session?** Names and roles, up to three colleagues. Please include at least one person who can agree to owners and dates on the day.

**Anything I should know that the questions did not ask?**

---

One request: please do not send confidential documents or any information that identifies individual patients or family members. I do not need them for this session.
