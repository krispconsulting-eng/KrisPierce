# Ongoing Strategic Advisor: onboarding kit

> **What this is:** the first 30 days of every retainer, the intake questionnaire, and the one-page advisory brief. Onboarding time comes out of month 1 of the retainer; there is no separate fee.

## 1. First-30-days plan

| When | What happens | Who | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Before day 1 | Conflict check completed and recorded in the proposal. Agreement signed. First invoice paid. | Me, client | Conflict check record |
| Day 1 | Welcome email sent with the intake questionnaire and the document list (see `emails.md`) | Me | |
| Days 1 to 5 | Client returns the questionnaire and documents | Client | Completed questionnaire |
| Days 3 to 7 | I read everything. If there is a CEOF Diagnostic result I review it; if not, the client completes one | Me | Reading notes; CEOF baseline |
| Days 5 to 10 | Onboarding session: 90 minutes (1.5-day tier) or 2 hours (3-day tier). Agenda below | Me, client lead, decision owners | Draft advisory brief |
| Within 3 working days | Advisory brief sent for confirmation | Me | Advisory brief v1 |
| Days 10 to 15 | Client confirms the brief. Monthly session dates set for the full term. Quarterly review dates set | Client | Confirmed brief; calendar |
| Days 10 to 20 | Up to 3 short introductory calls (20 minutes each) with people named in the brief. 3-day tier only | Me | Notes |
| Days 15 to 25 | First document review, to set the pattern | Both | First review memo |
| Days 25 to 30 | First monthly working session | Both | First monthly advisory note |
| Day 30 | Check: is the brief right, is the rhythm working, anything to change? Five minutes at the end of the first session | Both | Any changes noted |

For a client arriving from a sprint or a design project, the onboarding session is shortened to 60 minutes and starts from the decision paper or framework already delivered.

**Documents to ask for (up to 8):**
1. Current strategy or plan, the part that touches consumers.
2. Engagement framework, policy or plan, if any.
3. Terms of reference for any consumer or community advisory group.
4. Payment and recognition policy for consumers, if any.
5. Organisation chart for the people involved.
6. The last two board or executive papers that mention consumer engagement.
7. CEOF Diagnostic report and any debrief action plan.
8. Anything showing what happened after consumers were last consulted.

Company clients: nothing that names a product for promotional purposes, nothing relating to a submission or application, and nothing that identifies an individual consumer.

**Onboarding session agenda**

| Time | Item |
|---|---|
| 10 minutes | How this works: what I do, what I do not do, how to reach me, how time is counted |
| 30 minutes | The three decisions: what they are, who owns each, when each is needed, what is already settled |
| 15 minutes | People: who matters to these decisions, inside and outside |
| 15 minutes | Constraints: money, time, policy, history, what has been tried |
| 10 minutes | What good looks like in six months |
| 10 minutes | Boundaries: the independence line, what happens if a conflict arises, confidentiality |

(3-day tier: the extra 30 minutes goes on the three decisions.)

## 2. Intake questionnaire

Sent with the welcome email. About 20 minutes to complete. Short answers are fine.

**About you**
1. Organisation, your name and role.
2. Who else will attend the monthly sessions? Names and roles.
3. Who signs off on engagement decisions?

**Where things stand**
4. In two or three sentences, how does your organisation involve consumers today?
5. Have you completed the CEOF Diagnostic? If yes, what were your three lowest domains?
6. What consumer engagement work is under way or planned in the next six months?
7. Do you pay consumers for their time? At what rates, under which published schedule, and how quickly?
8. What has gone badly in the past, and what did you learn?

**The next six months**
9. What are the three decisions in the next six months where outside advice would change what you do? For each: the decision, who makes it, and the date.
10. What is already decided and not open to change?
11. What deadlines, funding rounds, board dates or external commitments fall in this period?
12. What documents do you expect to ask me to review?

**People and constraints**
13. Who inside the organisation has to be persuaded for these decisions to stick?
14. Which outside organisations or groups have a say or an interest?
15. What are your constraints: budget, staffing, policy, timing?

**Working together**
16. How do you prefer to be reached between sessions: email, phone, messages?
17. What would make you say in six months that this was worth it?
18. What would make you end it early?

**For the conflict check**
19. Does your organisation have a product, technology or service that is, or is likely to be, the subject of an application to MSAC, PBAC or another government advisory committee in the next 12 months?
20. Are you in any negotiation, partnership or dispute with Rare Diseases NSW or UNSW, SCN2A Australia, or any organisation you know I work with?
21. Is there anything else I should know before I confirm there is no conflict?

## 3. Advisory brief template (one page)

Written by me after the onboarding session, confirmed by the client, reviewed at every quarterly review. It must fit on one page. If it does not, there are too many decisions.

>

>

> |---|---|---|---|---|
> | 1 | [for example: How should consumers be involved in setting the 2027-28 priorities?] | [name, role] | [date] | [text] |
> | 2 | [decision] | [name, role] | [date] | [text] |
> | 3 | [decision] | [name, role] | [date] | [text] |

>

>

>

>
> Conflict check completed [date]: [no conflict identified / managed as follows]. Next check: [date of first quarterly review].

**Internal checks before the brief goes out**
- [ ] Each decision is a question with an owner and a date.
- [ ] None of the three decisions touches a submission or application.
- [ ] "What good looks like" could be checked by someone outside the organisation.
- [ ] The brief fits on one page.
- [ ] Session and review dates are in the calendar for the full term.
- [ ] Retainer recorded in the pipeline tracker; capacity rule checked (no more than two retainers).
