**Between** Kris Pierce Consulting (ABN [ABN]) **and** [client: organisation]. **Proposal reference:** [KPC-year-number].

This page summarises the main terms in plain language. The full consulting agreement sets out the terms in detail. If this summary and the full agreement differ, the full agreement applies. [Lawyer to confirm this sentence.]

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **What you are buying** | An independent adviser on consumer engagement for the decisions named in your advisory brief. I advise. Your team does the work and makes the decisions. |
| **Size and fee** | [1.5 days (12 hours) a month at $3,500 / 3 days (24 hours) a month at $6,500], excluding GST. Time is counted in quarter hours and reported to you every month. |
| **Term** | Six months minimum, from [start date] to [end date]. After that it continues month to month unless renewed for a further fixed term. |
| **Each month you get** | One working session ([90 minutes / 3 hours, or two of 90 minutes]); up to [2 / 4] document reviews of up to [20 / 30] pages each, returned as a memo within [5 / 3] working days of a complete request; advice between sessions with a reply within [2 working days / 1 working day] and calls of up to 30 minutes; a one-page advisory note within 3 working days of the session; a statement of time used. |
| **Each quarter** | A review at months 3 and 6 of what changed because of the advice, the time used, and whether to renew, resize or stop. I re-run my conflict check each quarter and will ask whether anything has changed on your side. |
| **Not included** | Writing your documents or producing deliverables. Running, recruiting for or holding data from sessions with consumers. Review of promotional material. Speaking for you or making introductions. Urgent, same-day or out-of-hours response. Medical, legal or regulatory advice. |
| **Never included** | Product-specific work on any application that is, or is likely to be, before a committee I sit on or advise, including any part of a submission, and any advice on how a committee is likely to respond. Access to, or information about, any public body. |
| **Unused time** | Carries over for one month only, up to [4 / 8] hours, and is used first. After that it lapses. It is not refunded and does not carry past the end of the term. Time lost because I was unavailable carries over in full. [Kris: confirm.] |
| **Extra time** | Up to one extra day in any month at $2,400, agreed in writing before the work is done. Nothing is billed after the fact. |
| **Separate work** | If you need something built, I will say so and quote it separately as a fixed-price project. |
| **Invoices** | Monthly in advance, payable within 14 days. Expenses only if agreed in writing beforehand. |
| **Changing size** | Up at any time, from the start of the next month. Down at a quarterly review, from the start of the next month. |
| **Pause** | One pause of up to one month in the term, by agreement, with the term extended by the same period. [Kris: confirm.] |
| **Ending** | After the first six months, either of us can end it with 30 days' written notice. If you end it inside the first six months, the remaining months of the minimum term are payable unless I agree otherwise in writing. [Lawyer to confirm wording.] If I end it, you pay only for work done to that date and any fee paid in advance for unused time is refunded. |
| **Conflicts** | If your circumstances change in a way that could create a conflict for me, for example a product moving towards an application to a government advisory committee, please tell me straight away. I may need to pause, change the scope or end the retainer, on 30 days' notice or less where the conflict requires it. |
| **Declarations** | Where my other roles require it, I will declare this engagement and the fee. By signing you agree to that. |
| **Confidentiality** | Each of us keeps the other's confidential information confidential, during the retainer and afterwards. I do not tell other clients about you, or you about them. I will not name you as a client without your written consent. |
| **Your documents** | Stay yours, are used only for this work, and are returned or deleted within 30 days of the end. Please send nothing that identifies an individual consumer or patient. |
| **Ownership** | You own the memos and notes I write for you. I keep my methods, templates and tools, including the CEOF Diagnostic, and you may use them inside your organisation as they appear in what I give you. |

**Medicines Australia Code of Conduct.** This is a consultancy under a written agreement. The fee is fair market value for the work and is not connected to any product, prescribing or purchasing decision. The work is non-promotional: I do not review promotional material or make product claims. Contact with consumers stays at arm's length: I do not introduce you to individual consumers, and no individual consumer data passes between us. Kris Pierce Consulting is a business, not a patient organisation, and payments to it should not be reported as support for one. You may disclose this engagement where the Code or your policies require it.

**Independence.** My government advisory appointments are held independently of this practice. Clients receive no access to those bodies or their deliberations.

**Consumers.** No consumer participation is planned in this retainer, so no consumer payments apply. Where your own work involves consumers, they are paid by you at published rates as a separate line in your budget.
