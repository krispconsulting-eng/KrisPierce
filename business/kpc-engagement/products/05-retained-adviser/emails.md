# Ongoing Strategic Advisor: emails

> **What this is:** the five standard emails for a retainer. Sent from info@krispierce.com.au. Fill in the [square brackets]. Nothing is sent without Kris's approval.

---

## 1. Welcome (on acceptance)

**Subject:** Starting on [date]: how the next six months will work

Hi [name],

Thank you for accepting the proposal. The retainer runs from [start date] to [end date], at [1.5 / 3] days a month.

Here is how it works.

**Every month**
- A working session of [90 minutes / 3 hours, or two of 90 minutes]. I suggest [day and time]; tell me if another slot suits better and I will set dates for the full term.
- Up to [2 / 4] document reviews, each returned as a short memo within [5 / 3] working days.
- Advice between sessions. Email me or call [number]; I reply within [2 / 1] working [days / day].
- A one-page advisory note after each session: what was decided, what I advised, who is doing what, and what to watch.

**Every quarter**
A review of what has changed because of the advice, the time used, and whether to renew, resize or stop. Dates: [month 3 date] and [month 6 date].

**This week, I need three things from you**
1. The attached intake questionnaire, by [date]. About 20 minutes.
2. The documents on the attached list, or as many as exist.
3. A time for the onboarding session, [90 minutes / 2 hours], between [date] and [date]. Please bring the people who own the decisions.

From that session I will write a one-page advisory brief: the three decisions that matter this half-year, the people, the constraints and what good looks like in six months. Everything I do afterwards is measured against it.

**What I do and do not do**
I advise; your team decides and delivers. If you need something built, I will say so and scope it as a separate piece of work, so there are no surprise invoices. I do not work on anything connected to a submission or application before a committee I sit on or advise.

My government advisory appointments are held independently of this practice. Clients receive no access to those bodies or their deliberations.

If your circumstances change in a way that could create a conflict, please tell me straight away.

I am looking forward to starting.

Best regards,
Kris

**Attachments:** intake questionnaire; document list; document review request form.

## 3. Quarterly review invitation

**Subject:** Quarterly review on [date]: has the advice changed anything?

Hi [name],

The retainer reaches [three / six] months on [date], so it is time for the quarterly review. I have booked [60 / 90] minutes on [day, date, time]. [Link.] Please include [decision owner names].

The review answers four questions:

1. What changed because of the advice?
2. How was the time used?
3. What matters most next quarter?
4. Should this continue as it is, change size, or stop?

All three options in question 4 are open every time. I will give you my recommendation and the reason, and the decision is yours.

Attached is my draft of the review, built from the monthly notes and the time log. Before the meeting, please:

- Read section 1 and add your own view of what changed. Where nothing changed, say so.
- Answer the questions in section 4 on how the arrangement is working.
- Tell me whether anything has changed that could create a conflict: a product moving towards an application to a government advisory committee, or a new negotiation or partnership.

Best regards,
Kris

**Attachment:** quarterly review, draft.
