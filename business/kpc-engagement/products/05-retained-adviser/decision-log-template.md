# Ongoing Strategic Advisor: decision log template

> **What this is:** the client-facing running log of a retainer: each decision discussed, the advice I gave, what the client decided, and the outcome when it is known. One log per client, kept for the whole term and sent with every monthly advisory note.
> **How it differs from the other records:** the monthly advisory note (`monthly-rhythm.md`, section 2) is one page about one month. This log is cumulative, so that at month 3 and month 6 the question "what changed because of the advice?" can be answered from a record, not from memory. It feeds section 1 of `quarterly-review-template.md`. My own time and working notes are in `time-and-advice-log.csv`, which the client does not receive.
> **How to use it:** I add rows within 3 working days of each session and after any on-call advice of substance. The client fills the "what you decided" and "outcome" columns, or tells me and I enter their words. Fill in the [square brackets]. Nothing is sent without Kris's approval.

## How to read this log

- I advise. Your team decides. The two are in separate columns on purpose, and I never fill in yours for you.
- Where you chose a different course from my advice, it is recorded without judgement. Sometimes you will have been right.
- "Nothing changed" and "not yet known" are acceptable entries. An empty outcome column is not: it gets a review date.
- "How sure" is my own confidence in the advice: sure, fairly sure, or a best guess. I say which.
