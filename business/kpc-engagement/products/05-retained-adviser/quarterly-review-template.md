# Ongoing Strategic Advisor: quarterly review template

> **What this is:** the review held at month 3 and month 6 of every six-month term: 60 minutes on the 1.5-day tier, 90 minutes on the 3-day tier. It replaces nothing; the monthly session still runs. Its purpose is one decision: renew, resize or stop.
> **Preparation:** I complete sections 1 to 4 in draft from the monthly advisory notes and the time log and send it 5 working days ahead. The client adds their view. On the 3-day tier, the completed template is the written quarter summary (up to 2 pages).

## Before the review

- [ ] Re-run the standing conflict check (`conflict-and-boundaries.md`, section 2). Record the date and result in section 6 below.
- [ ] Collate the three monthly advisory notes and the time log.
- [ ] Send the invitation and the draft (see `emails.md`).
- [ ] Ask the client to come with their own answer to: what changed because of the advice?

## 5. Renew, resize or stop

*All three options are discussed every time. I give my recommendation first, with the reason, and the client decides.*

| Option | When it is the right call | What happens next |
|---|---|---|
| **Renew** at the same tier | Advice is changing decisions; time is about right; there are decisions ahead that warrant it | Quarter 1: continue. Quarter 2: new six-month term or month to month; new advisory brief |
| **Resize up** (to 3 days) | Time is consistently short; extra days are being bought; the wider team needs to be in the room | Takes effect from the start of next month |
| **Resize down** (to 1.5 days) | Time is consistently under-used; the decisions are fewer or further off | Takes effect from the start of next month |
| **Convert to a project** | The need has become a deliverable | I quote a Strategic Advisory Sprint or Consumer Engagement Design |
| **Stop** | The decisions are made; the team no longer needs the outside view; the advice is not changing decisions | Quarter 1: the minimum term still applies unless I agree to release it, which I will where the retainer is plainly not working [Kris: confirm]. Quarter 2: final session and closing note |
