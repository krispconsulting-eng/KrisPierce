# Ongoing Strategic Advisor: monthly rhythm

> **What this is:** what happens every month of a retainer, with the four templates that carry it: the working session agenda, the monthly advisory note, the document review request form, and the review memo.

## The month at a glance

| When | What | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 5 working days before the session | Session reminder sent, asking for agenda items and papers (see `emails.md`) | Me |
| 2 working days before | Client sends items and any papers | Client |
| Session day | Monthly working session: 90 minutes (1.5-day tier); 3 hours, or two sessions of 90 minutes (3-day tier) | Both |
| Within 3 working days | Monthly advisory note sent, with the time statement | Me |
| Any time | Document reviews by request form; on-call advice | Both |
| Last working day | Time log closed; carry-over calculated; conflict prompt (two questions, below) | Me |

**End-of-month conflict prompt (for me, two minutes):**
1. Did anything this month touch a product, application or committee matter? If yes, see `conflict-and-boundaries.md`.
2. Did anything I learned from this client bear on another client, or the reverse?

**Time log:** one line per item, in quarter hours: date, what, time. Kept by me, summarised on the advisory note each month.

## 1. Working session agenda template

Same shape every month. The session is for decisions, not updates. Updates go in writing beforehand.

> **[Organisation]: advisory session, [month, year]**
> [Date, time, link]. Attending: [names].
>
> | Time (90-minute session) | Item |
> |---|---|
> | 0:00 to 0:10 | **Since last month.** Actions from the last note: done, not done, why. What changed as a result. |
> | 0:10 to 0:55 | **The decisions.** One, two or all three from the advisory brief, in the order of the nearest date. For each: where it stands, the options, what is in the way, my advice. |
> | 0:55 to 1:10 | **One live problem.** Something that came up this month that is not on the brief. |
> | 1:10 to 1:20 | **Watch list.** What is coming in the next 60 days: dates, risks, people. |
> | 1:20 to 1:30 | **Actions and close.** Who does what by when. Documents coming for review next month. Next session date. |
>
> **Items for this session (client to add, 2 working days before):**
> 1. [item]
> 2. [item]
>
> **Papers attached:** [list]

**3-hour session (3-day tier):** the same shape with the decisions block extended to 100 minutes, a 10-minute break, and a 40-minute working block on one decision with the wider team (for example: testing a draft plan, preparing for a board discussion, rehearsing a conversation with a partner). If the client prefers two 90-minute sessions, the second is a working block on one decision.

**My preparation (30 to 60 minutes):** re-read the advisory brief, last month's note and the watch list; read papers; write down the one thing I think the client is not seeing.

**Rules I keep in the session:**
- Say what I think, early, and say how sure I am.
- Separate what I know from what I am inferring.
- If a question is outside what I will advise on, say so then, not later.
- End with actions that have names and dates against them.

## 2. Monthly advisory note template (one page)

Sent within 3 working days of the session. One page. It is the record of the retainer: over six months, the six notes show what the advice changed.

> # Advisory note: [organisation], [month, year]
>
> Session held [date]. Attending: [names]. Month [number] of [6].
>
> ## Decisions discussed
>
> | Decision (from the advisory brief) | Where it stands | Decided this month |
> |---|---|---|
> | 1. [short title] | [one line] | [what was decided, or "not yet; needed by date"] |
> | 2. [short title] | [one line] | [text] |
> | 3. [short title] | [one line] | [text] |
> | Other: [topic] | [one line] | [text] |
>
> ## Advice given
>
> 1. [The advice, in one or two sentences, with the reason.]
> 2. [Advice.]
> 3. [Advice.]
>
> Where you chose a different course from my advice: [what, and why, recorded without judgement].
>
> ## Actions
>
> | Action | Owner | By |
> |---|---|---|
> | [action] | [name] | [date] |
> | [action] | [name] | [date] |
> | [action: mine] | Kris | [date] |
>
> ## Watch list
>
> | What | Why it matters | When |
> |---|---|---|
> | [date, risk, person or external change] | [one line] | [date or window] |
>
> ## Time this month
>
> | | Hours |
> |---|---|
> | Included this month | [12 / 24] |
> | Carried in from last month | [0] |
> | Used: session and note [x]; reviews [x]; on-call [x]; other [x] | [total] |
> | Carried to next month (limit [4 / 8] hours, one month only) | [x] |
> | Lapsed | [x] |
>
> Document reviews this month: [number] of [2 / 4]. Next session: [date].
>
> Best regards,
> Kris

**Checks before sending**
- [ ] One page.
- [ ] Every action has an owner and a date.
- [ ] Advice is stated plainly enough that someone who was not in the session could act on it.
- [ ] Nothing in the note is about a submission, an application or a committee.
- [ ] No individual consumer is identifiable.
- [ ] Time adds up.

## 3. Document review request form

The client completes this for every review. The turnaround clock starts when the form and the document have both arrived and the form is complete.

> **Document review request**
>
> 1. **Document title and version:** [text]
> 2. **Type:** [engagement plan / consumer advisory group terms of reference / payment and recognition policy / participant-facing material / consumer involvement section of a grant application / board or executive paper / partnership proposal / evaluation plan / other]
> 3. **Length:** [pages] (limit: [20 / 30] pages per review; longer documents count as two)
> 4. **Which decision in the advisory brief does it serve?** [1 / 2 / 3 / none]
> 5. **Who is it for, and what do you want them to do after reading it?** [text]
> 6. **What stage is it at?** [early draft / near final / final check]
> 7. **What do you most want my view on?** Up to three questions. [text]
> 8. **What is fixed and not open to comment?** [text]
> 9. **When do you need it?** [date]. Standard turnaround is [5 / 3] working days.
> 10. **Who else is reviewing it?** [text]
>
> **Please confirm:**
> - [ ] This document is not part of, and is not being prepared for, a submission or application to MSAC, PBAC or another government advisory committee.
> - [ ] It is not promotional material and does not name a product for a promotional purpose.
> - [ ] It contains no information that identifies an individual consumer or patient.

**What I do on receipt**
1. Check the three confirmations. If any cannot be ticked, I do not open the document; I reply the same day and explain why (see `conflict-and-boundaries.md`).
2. Check the review count for the month and the page limit.
3. Confirm the return date by reply.

**In scope for review:** engagement strategies and plans; terms of reference; payment and recognition policies; recruitment and participant-facing material; consumer involvement sections of grant applications; board papers on engagement; partnership approaches; evaluation and measurement plans; role descriptions for engagement staff.

**Out of scope for review:** anything forming part of a submission or application to a committee I sit on or advise, including consumer input sections; promotional material; contracts and legal documents (I can comment on the engagement content only); clinical content.

## 4. Review memo template (up to two pages)

> # Review memo: [document title, version]
>
> For: [name], [organisation]. From: Kris Pierce. Date: [date]. Request received: [date].
>
> ## My view in three lines
>
> [Would this document do what you need it to do with the reader you named? Yes, not yet, or no, and the main reason.]
>
> ## Answers to your questions
>
> 1. **[Their question 1]** [answer]
> 2. **[Their question 2]** [answer]
> 3. **[Their question 3]** [answer]
>
> ## What to change, in order of importance
>
> | # | Where | Issue | Suggested change | Must fix or consider |
> |---|---|---|---|---|
> | 1 | [section or page] | [text] | [text] | [must fix] |
> | 2 | [section or page] | [text] | [text] | [consider] |
> | 3 | [section or page] | [text] | [text] | [consider] |
>
> ## What is working and should stay
>
> - [point]
> - [point]
>
> ## How a consumer would read this
>
> [Two or three sentences from the point of view of the people the document affects: what they would notice, what they would doubt, what is missing for them.]
>
> ## Against the seven CEOF domains (where relevant)
>
> [Name the one or two domains this document most affects, using the domain names exactly, and say whether it moves the organisation forward on them.]
>
> ## What I did not review
>
> [Anything outside my scope or expertise: legal wording, clinical content, budget figures, anything marked as fixed.]
>
> Time used: [hours]. Reviews used this month: [number] of [2 / 4].
>
> Best regards,
> Kris

**Rules for review memos**
- Lead with the view. The client should know in three lines whether to proceed.
- No more than seven changes. If there are more, the document needs a conversation, not a memo.
- I comment; I do not rewrite. One example of wording per issue at most.
- I say what I did not review.

## 5. On-call advice

| | 1.5-day tier | 3-day tier |
|---|---|---|
| Reply within | 2 working days | 1 working day |
| Call length | Up to 30 minutes | Up to 30 minutes |
| Good uses | A quick test of a decision before a meeting; how to respond to a concern from a consumer group; whether something needs a fuller conversation | Same |
| Not for | Emergencies; drafting; anything excluded in PRODUCT.md section 4 | Same |

Every on-call exchange gets one line in the time log and, if it produced advice of substance, one line in that month's advisory note.
