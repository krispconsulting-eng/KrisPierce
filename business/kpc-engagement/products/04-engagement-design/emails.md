# Consumer Engagement Design: emails

> **What this is:** the four standard emails for a Consumer Engagement Design project. Sent from info@krispierce.com.au. Fill in the [square brackets]. Nothing is sent without Kris's approval.

---

## 1. Kickoff (to the client, on acceptance)

**Subject:** [Project name]: starting [date], and what I need from you this week

Hi [name],

Thank you for accepting the proposal. The work starts on [date] and finishes with handover on [date].

The decision this work serves: **[decision, as written in the proposal]**. Everything I do over the next [6 / 8 / 12] weeks is there to inform it.

What happens first:

1. **Kickoff call**, 60 minutes, with you as decision owner. Please choose a time: [options].
2. **Discovery interviews.** I need [4 / 6 / 10] people for 45 minutes each, between [date] and [date]. Please send names, roles and email addresses by [date].
3. **Documents.** Up to [5 / 10 / 15]: your current engagement plan or policy, terms of reference for any consumer group, your payment policy if you have one, the last two reports to your board or funder that mention consumers, and anything that shows what happened after you last consulted.
4. **Recruitment.** I will send the recruitment brief and the participant pack by [date]. The consent wording needs your legal review. Recruitment needs three weeks, so I need your approval by [date].

Two things to settle on the kickoff call:

- **What is not open to influence.** Participants will be told at the start, so I need an honest list.
- **Consumer payments.** The proposal sets these at $[amount] under [schedule name], as a separate line from my fee. I need to know who makes the payments and that they can be made within 14 days of each session.

[Company clients: A reminder of the conditions in the proposal. Recruitment runs through [independent party]. Your team will not attend consumer sessions or receive anything that identifies a participant. No product is named or discussed.]

Your point of contact is me. If your circumstances change in a way that could create a conflict, please tell me straight away.

Best regards,
Kris

## 2. Participant invitation (to a selected participant)

**Subject:** A place for you in the [organisation] sessions

Hi [name],

Thank you for putting your name forward. I would like to offer you a place.

**What it is:** [2 / 3] small group sessions about [decision, in plain words], with up to [7] other people who have lived experience of [condition or community]. I am running them independently on behalf of [organisation].

**When:**
- Session 1: [day, date, time]
- Session 2: [day, date, time]
- [Session 3: [day, date, time]]

**Where:** [online; I will send a link and a phone number / venue and address].

**Payment:** $[234] for each session, and $[50] for an hour reading a summary afterwards, paid by [who] within 14 days of each session. Costs of taking part, such as travel or care, are covered.

**What I need from you by [date]:**
1. A reply to say yes or no. Either is fine.
2. The attached consent form, once you have read the information sheet. If you would like to talk it through first, I am happy to call you.
3. Anything that would make it easier to take part.

A few things worth knowing now. You do not have to share anything you would rather not. You can step out or stop at any time and still be paid. [Organisation] has been clear about what can and cannot change as a result of these sessions, and I will tell you that at the start.

[Company clients: This work is funded by [company]. The company will not be told who takes part and will not be in the sessions.]

I will be in touch individually before the first session so you know what to expect.

Best regards,
Kris

**Attachments:** participant information sheet; consent form; payment details form.

### Variant: no place this time

**Subject:** The [organisation] sessions: an update

Hi [name],

Thank you for putting your name forward for the [organisation] sessions. There were more people than places, and I have not been able to offer you one this time. I chose the group to get a mix of ages, locations and backgrounds, and that meant turning down people with a great deal to offer.

[If you would like to send me your thoughts in writing on [question], I will include them. / If you would like to hear about future opportunities with [organisation], reply and I will pass on your details, with your agreement.]

Best regards,
Kris

## 3. Participant thank you, with the report back

Two emails. The first within 24 hours of each session. The second with the report back at the end.

### 3a. After each session

**Subject:** Thank you for today

Hi [name],

Thank you for today. What you said about [general theme, nothing identifying] will go straight into the work.

- **Payment:** $[234] will be paid by [who] by [date]. If it has not arrived by then, tell me and I will chase it.
- **Anything to add:** if something occurs to you later, or there was something you did not want to say in the group, reply to this email or call me on [phone].
- **Next session:** [day, date, time]. [I will send a short summary 3 working days before.]

Sessions like this can stay with you afterwards. If today brought up something hard, Lifeline is on 13 11 14 and Carer Gateway is on 1800 422 737, and you are welcome to contact me.

Best regards,
Kris

### 3b. Final thank you, with the report back

**Subject:** What [organisation] heard, and what it has decided

Hi [name],

Attached is the summary I promised: what was heard across the sessions, what [organisation] will do, and what it will not do and why. It is signed by [decision owner, role], because the commitments in it are theirs.

The short version:

- **The decision:** [one sentence on what was decided].
- **What will change first:** [one or two actions with dates].
- **What will not change:** [one line, with the reason].

You helped check an earlier draft of this. The corrections people sent have been made.

What happens next: [organisation] will [update you in six months / publish a summary in month]. [If you opted in, [name, role] at [organisation] will be in touch about future opportunities. If you did not, your details will not be passed on.]

Your final payment of $[amount] will be made by [date]. Notes and recordings from the sessions will be deleted by [date], as set out in the information sheet.

If anything in the summary is wrong, or you have a concern about how the sessions were run, tell me, or contact [independent contact name and details].

Thank you for the time and thought you gave this.

Best regards,
Kris

**Attachment:** "What we heard, what we will do, what we will not do and why".
