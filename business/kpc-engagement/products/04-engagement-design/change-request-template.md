# Change request

> **What this is:** the written change used whenever something is added to, removed from or moved within a Consumer Engagement Design project after acceptance. The proposal says a change to the decision or the scope is agreed in writing and may change the fee and dates; this is that writing. No change is worked on until both parties have signed it. No excess is billed after the fact.
> **The rule on size:** the three sizes are fixed and are not blended. Extra sessions, participants, interviews or decision types move the project to the next size, or the item is parked in the first-year plan (`PRODUCT.md`, sections 3, 6 and 10).
