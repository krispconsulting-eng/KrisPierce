## What you get

In [6 / 8 / 12] weeks you will have an engagement framework your organisation can run without me: who you involve, in which decisions, at what level, how they are paid, how they are kept safe, and how you report back. It is built with consumers in the room.

*Keep one block.*

**Focused.** One program, project or grant. Up to 3 decision types. 4 discovery interviews and up to 5 documents reviewed. 2 co-design sessions of 2 hours with 6 consumer participants. Findings table and report back to participants. Framework sections 1 to 8 (up to 12 pages), measures for your three lowest CEOF domains, a 90-day plan, and a 60-minute handover session.

**Standard.** One organisation or one major program. Up to 6 decision types. 6 discovery interviews and up to 10 documents reviewed. 3 co-design sessions of 2 hours with 8 consumer participants. Findings table and report back to participants. Full framework (10 sections, up to 20 pages), measures across all seven CEOF domains, a 12-month plan by quarter, a standalone payment and recognition policy, a midpoint check, a 90-minute handover session and a follow-up call at 30 days.

**Full.** Whole organisation, or a multi-site or multi-partner program. Up to 10 decision types. 10 discovery interviews and up to 15 documents reviewed. 6 co-design sessions of 2 hours with 16 consumer participants in two groups. Findings table comparing the two groups, and report back to participants. Full framework (10 sections, up to 30 pages) with a costed first-year budget, measures across all seven CEOF domains with baselines, a 12-month plan with owners, a payment and recognition policy with a participant-facing version, a leadership or board briefing, a 90-minute handover session and follow-up calls at 30 and 90 days.
