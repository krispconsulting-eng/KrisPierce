# Risk and safeguarding log

> **What this is:** the log I open at kickoff and keep until close-out, for any engagement with patients, carers and family members. Ten typical risks are filled in with the response to each. Add project-specific risks below them. It sits beside the project risks in `PRODUCT.md`, section 10, which cover scope, recruitment numbers and client conduct; this log covers the safety of the people taking part.
> **How to use it:** review it before recruitment opens, before every session, and at close-out. Change the status column each time. An event that happens is recorded in the events table at the end, the same day.
> **Limits:** this is a facilitation log, not a clinical one. I am not a clinician in this role. [Lawyer to confirm any mandatory reporting obligations that apply in the client's state or territory.]
