# Co-design session record

> **What this is:** the record I complete after every co-design session, within 2 working days. It is the bridge between the session and the raw input log in `synthesis-method.md`. One record per session, per group. Internal: it stays with Kris Pierce Consulting. The client receives a summary only; a company client receives themes only.
> **Rules:** participant codes, never names. No diagnosis details beyond what is needed. Anything a participant asks to withdraw is deleted here and in the raw input log.
