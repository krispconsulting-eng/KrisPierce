# Consumer Engagement Design: close-out checklist

> **What this is:** the full close-out for a project, completed between the handover session and the day the file is closed. It expands the short list at the end of `phase-plan.md`; where they overlap, tick both. A project is not closed until every line has a date or a written reason.
> **Order matters:** participants first (report back, payments, their information), then the client (handover, invoice), then my own practice (naming, testimonial, follow-up, lessons).

**Project:** [name]. **Client:** [organisation]. **Size:** [Focused / Standard / Full]. **Handover held:** [date]. **File closed:** [date].

## 6. Permission to name the client

No client is named, and no work is described in a way that could be recognised, without written consent.

- [ ] Request sent. Date: [date]. Wording below.
- [ ] Answer: [yes, in writing, on date; filed at location / no / no reply: do not name].
- [ ] Scope of the consent recorded: [name only / name and a one-line description / a named case on the service page, text approved by the client on date].
- [ ] Company clients: the client has confirmed that being named is consistent with its own disclosure obligations. [Lawyer to confirm.]

**Wording for the naming request**

> Hi [name],
>
> With your written consent, I would like to be able to say that [organisation] is a client of Kris Pierce Consulting. I would use [the name only / the name and this one line: "[line]"]. I would not describe the findings, name any person, or imply that you endorse my work.
>
> If you are comfortable with that, a reply saying "yes" is enough. If you would prefer not, that is completely fine, and I will not name you. You can withdraw consent at any time and I will remove the reference within five working days.
>
> Best regards,
> Kris

## 7. Testimonial request, with consent

Ask only after the 30-day point, or after handover for Focused, and only if the work changed a decision. A testimonial is the client's own words. I do not draft it for them, edit its meaning or offer anything in return.

- [ ] Request sent. Date: [date].
- [ ] Reply received. Date: [date]. Exact words filed at [location].
- [ ] Consent recorded for each use: [website / proposals / LinkedIn]. Name and role shown as: [text, or "organisation type only"].
- [ ] Final text shown to the client as it will appear, and approved. Date: [date].
- [ ] No participant is quoted as a testimonial. Participant words are used only under the quote consent in the information sheet.

**Wording for the testimonial request**

> Hi [name],
>
> Now that the framework has been running for [a month / a few weeks], I have a request. You are welcome to say no.
>
> Would you be willing to write two or three sentences, in your own words, about what changed for [organisation] as a result of the work? The most useful thing for someone considering it is the decision it helped you make and what is different now.
>
> If you are, please also tell me:
>
> 1. where I may use it: my website, my proposals, LinkedIn, or only some of these;
> 2. how you would like to be described: your name and role, your role only, or the type of organisation only.
>
> I will show you exactly how it will appear before it is used anywhere, and you can change your mind at any time. I will not edit your words beyond length, and only with your approval.
>
> Best regards,
> Kris
