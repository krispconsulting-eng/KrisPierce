# Consumer recruitment and payment kit

> **What this is:** everything needed to recruit, inform, pay and look after consumer participants in a Consumer Engagement Design project. Fill in the [square brackets]. The participant information sheet and consent wording are drafts and must be reviewed by the client's lawyer, and mine, before first use.

Contents: 1. Recruitment brief. 2. Expression-of-interest wording. 3. Participant information sheet and consent. 4. Payment schedule. 5. Example participant cost lines. 6. Accessibility and safety checklist. 7. Distress protocol.

|---|---|---|
| Meeting of up to four hours | $234 per meeting | Each 2-hour co-design session |

| One-off work | $50 an hour | Reviewing the report back (1 hour); any pre-reading over 30 minutes |

Before quoting these figures to a client or participant, check them against the current Health Consumers Queensland position statement. The figures above were taken from a search summary recorded in `../../../research/01-ai-and-consulting-market.md`, not from the primary document, and rates are revised from time to time.

### Other published schedules a client may already use

If the client has its own published schedule, use the client's, provided it is not lower than the default for the same time. Name the schedule in the proposal either way.

| Schedule | Published rate | Note |
|---|---|---|
| NSW Health | $40 an hour; $60 an hour for chairing | A 2-hour session at this rate is $80, lower than the default |
| Monash Partners (2022) | Member $55 an hour; chair $60 an hour | May be dated |
| Safer Care Victoria, A guide to consumer remuneration (December 2024) | See the guide | Rates not recorded in my research notes; read the guide before citing |

These are floors for lay consumers. They are not consultant rates and not my fee.

### Payment rules

1. Payment is costed as a separate line in the proposal (proposal template, section 7). It is never taken from my fee.
2. Participants are paid for every session they attend, in full, including if they leave early.
3. A participant who is confirmed and whose session is cancelled with less than 48 hours' notice is paid for it.
4. Payment within 14 days of each session, by bank transfer unless the participant asks for another method.
5. Expenses (travel, parking, care costs, data, access needs) are reimbursed at cost on top of the sitting payment.
6. Participants are told that payment may affect tax or income support, and are offered the choice to decline or vary it.
7. Who pays: the client directly, or Kris Pierce Consulting on the client's behalf, invoiced at cost with no margin. For company clients, never the company directly.
8. A payment log is kept: participant code (not name, in anything shared with the client), session, amount, date paid.

## 6. Accessibility and safety checklist

Run before every project and again before every session.

**Before recruitment**
- [ ] Expression of interest readable at about Year 8 level; available by phone as well as form.
- [ ] Access needs asked at expression of interest and again at confirmation.
- [ ] Session times offered outside school pick-up, and at least one evening option.
- [ ] What is not open to influence is stated up front.
- [ ] Funder named in plain words.

**Before each session**
- [ ] Information sheet sent at least seven days ahead; consent received from everyone.
- [ ] Each participant contacted individually: what to expect, how to step out, who the second person is.
- [ ] Materials sent 3 working days ahead, in an accessible format (plain document, large print on request, screen-reader friendly).
- [ ] Interpreter, captioning or Auslan booked where asked for.
- [ ] Online: platform tested with anyone unsure; phone dial-in available; cameras optional; private chat to the second person explained.
- [ ] In person: step-free access, accessible toilet, quiet room, parking or transport paid, food that meets stated needs, seating that allows people to leave without crossing the room.
- [ ] Second person confirmed (not the facilitator, not a senior staff member of the client; for company clients, not a company employee).
- [ ] Support contacts on the screen or table.
- [ ] Break of at least 10 minutes in the middle.

**During**
- [ ] Spoken consent check read aloud.
- [ ] Group agreement set in the first 10 minutes.
- [ ] No one is asked to tell their story as an entry ticket; people share what they choose.
- [ ] No clinical questions answered; nothing said is medical advice.
- [ ] Finish on time.

**After**
- [ ] Message to every participant within 24 hours: thanks, payment date, support contacts, how to add anything they did not say.
- [ ] Individual follow-up with anyone who stepped out or seemed affected.
- [ ] Payment triggered within 2 working days.
- [ ] Notes stored securely; recording deleted on the promised date.

## 7. Distress protocol

For me and the second person. Read it before every session. This is a facilitation protocol, not a clinical one.

**Roles**
- Facilitator (me): runs the session, watches the group, makes the call to pause.
- Second person: [name], watches individuals, takes anyone who needs it to a breakout or quiet room, stays with them.

**Signs to watch for:** tears or a shaking voice; going silent after being active; camera off suddenly; anger out of proportion to the topic; leaving without a word; talk of hopelessness.

**Step 1: notice and slow down.** Acknowledge without spotlighting: "That is a hard thing to say. Thank you. Take whatever time you need." Offer the pass: "You can stop there if you would like to."

**Step 2: offer a choice.** Carry on, pause, step out with the second person, or finish for the day. The participant decides. Payment is not affected and I say so.

**Step 3: pause the group if needed.** Call a 5-minute break for everyone. Do not ask the group to discuss what happened.

**Step 4: one to one.** The second person stays with the participant, listens, does not advise, and asks: "What would help right now?" and "Is there someone you would like me to call?"

**Step 5: if there is risk of harm.** If a participant says anything suggesting they or someone else may be at risk of serious harm: stay with them (or on the line); ask directly whether they are safe; give Lifeline 13 11 14; if danger is immediate, call 000. Tell them what I am doing and why. Record what was said and done, the same day.

**Step 6: follow up.** Contact the participant within 24 hours. Ask whether they want to continue, and whether they want their contribution from that session kept or removed. Their choice stands.

**Step 7: record and review.** A short incident note: date, what happened, what was done, follow-up. No more detail than needed. Shared with the client only in de-identified form, unless safety requires otherwise. [Lawyer to confirm any mandatory reporting obligations that apply in the client's state or territory.]

**For the facilitators:** debrief with the second person for 10 minutes after every session. If a session was heavy, I do not run another the same day.

**What this protocol does not cover:** clinical care, counselling, or crisis services. I am not a clinician in this role and do not act as one.
