# Consumer Engagement Design: phase plan

> **What this is:** the delivery plan for all three sizes. Four phases, fixed days, fixed outputs. Use it to fill the timeline in the proposal and to run the project.

## Days by phase and size

| Phase | Focused (8 days) | Standard (12 days) | Full (18 days) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Discovery | 1.5 | 2.5 | 3.5 |
| 2. Co-design | 3.0 | 4.5 | 7.5 |
| 3. Synthesis | 1.5 | 2.0 | 3.0 |
| 4. Framework and handover | 2.0 | 3.0 | 4.0 |
| **Total** | **8.0** | **12.0** | **18.0** |

If a phase runs over, the time comes out of a later phase or out of my own margin, not from the client. If it runs over because the scope changed, that is a written change (see PRODUCT.md, section 10).

## Elapsed time

| Phase | Focused | Standard | Full |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Discovery | Weeks 1 to 2 | Weeks 1 to 2 | Weeks 1 to 3 |
| 2. Co-design (recruitment opens in week 1) | Weeks 2 to 4 | Weeks 3 to 6 | Weeks 3 to 9 |
| 3. Synthesis | Week 5 | Weeks 6 to 7 | Weeks 9 to 10 |
| 4. Framework and handover | Week 6 | Week 8 | Weeks 11 to 12 |

Recruitment needs at least three weeks from the expression of interest going out to the first session. Start it in week 1.

## Phase 1: Discovery

**Purpose:** find out where engagement stands, which decisions are in scope, and what the organisation is prepared to change.

| Activity | Focused | Standard | Full |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kickoff call with the decision owner (60 minutes) | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| CEOF Diagnostic completed or existing result reviewed | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Document review | Up to 5 | Up to 10 | Up to 15 |
| Discovery interviews, 45 minutes each | 4 | 6 | 10 |
| Decision inventory: list each decision type in scope, its owner and its date | Up to 3 | Up to 6 | Up to 10 |
| Recruitment brief and participant pack tailored and sent for approval | Yes | Yes | Yes |

**Interview guide (same eight questions every time):**

1. Which decisions in the next 12 months should consumers shape?
2. Where have consumers changed a decision here before? What made that possible?
3. Where was input gathered and not used? Why?
4. Who is missing from the people you usually hear from?
5. What are you not prepared to change, whatever is said?
6. What does the organisation pay consumers now, and how quickly?
7. What has gone wrong before: harm, complaints, people walking away?
8. What would make you say in a year that this worked?

**Outputs:** discovery summary (2 pages); decision inventory; approved recruitment brief and participant pack; CEOF baseline.

**Decision point 1 (end of discovery), with the decision owner:**
- Are the decisions in scope the right ones, and are they open to influence? If a decision is already made, it comes out of scope and is labelled "inform only".
- What is off the table? Written down now, so participants are told the truth at the start.
- Payment schedule and payment route confirmed.
- Go or stop on recruitment.

## Phase 2: Co-design

**Purpose:** work with consumers on the experience, the priorities and the draft approach.

| Activity | Focused | Standard | Full |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recruitment: brief out, expressions of interest screened, selection, confirmations | 6 participants | 8 participants | 16 participants in two groups |
| Pre-session call or message with each participant (access needs, what to expect) | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Session 1: understanding experience | Yes, with shortened priority exercise | Yes | Yes, both groups |
| Session 2: setting priorities | No (folded into session 1) | Yes | Yes, both groups |
| Session 3: testing the draft approach | Yes | Yes | Yes, both groups |
| Session notes written up within 2 working days | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Payment triggered after each session | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Midpoint check with the decision owner | By email | 30 minutes | 45 minutes |

How the co-design days are spent:

| | Focused (3.0) | Standard (4.5) | Full (7.5) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recruitment, selection and participant contact | 1.0 | 1.5 | 1.5 |
| Sessions: preparation, facilitation and write-up, about 1 day per session | 2.0 | 3.0 | 6.0 |

**Outputs:** session notes for each session; raw input log (see `synthesis-method.md`); a draft approach taken into session 3.

**Decision point 2 (midpoint, after the priorities are set):**
- I share the emerging priorities with the decision owner, with no names attached.
- The decision owner says which priorities the organisation can act on, which it cannot, and why. Those reasons go to participants in session 3.
- Anything the organisation rules out here is recorded for the "what we will not do and why" section.

Between session 2 and session 3 (or between sessions 1 and 3 for Focused) I draft the approach: levels of involvement by decision type, the calendar, payment and feedback loops. Session 3 tests that draft.

## Phase 3: Synthesis

**Purpose:** turn raw input into findings and decisions the organisation can defend.

| Activity | Focused | Standard | Full |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coding of all input against the seven CEOF domains and open codes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Findings table | Up to 10 findings | Up to 15 findings | Up to 20 findings, with a comparison of the two groups |
| Minority views register | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Draft "What we heard, what we will do, what we will not do and why" | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Participant check of the report back (1 paid hour each, by email or a short call) | Yes | Yes | Yes |

**Outputs:** findings table; minority views register; draft report back, checked by participants.

**Decision point 3 (end of synthesis), with the decision owner:**
- For each finding: accept, accept in part, or decline with a reason.
- Sign-off on the wording of the report back. The organisation, not me, makes the commitments in it.
- Confirm the date the report back goes to participants.

## Phase 4: Framework and handover

**Purpose:** give the organisation a framework it can run, and hand it over properly.

| Activity | Focused | Standard | Full |
|---|---|---|---|
| Draft framework from `engagement-framework-template.md` | Sections 1 to 8 | All 10 sections | All 10 sections with budget |
| One round of consolidated comments | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Measures mapped to the seven CEOF domains | Three lowest | All seven | All seven with baselines |
| First-year plan | 90 days | 12 months | 12 months with owners and budget |
| Report back sent to participants | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Leadership or board briefing | No | No | 60 minutes |
| Handover session with the team who will run it | 60 minutes | 90 minutes | 90 minutes |
| Follow-up calls | None | 30 days | 30 and 90 days |

**Handover session agenda:**

1. The decision this work served, and what changed (10 minutes).
2. Walk through the framework, section by section (30 to 40 minutes).
3. The first quarter: who does what by when (15 minutes).
4. What will go wrong first, and what to do (10 minutes).
5. What I am no longer doing from today (5 minutes).

**Outputs:** final framework; payment and recognition policy; first-year plan; report back sent; handover held.

**Decision point 4 (handover), with the decision owner:**
- Adopt the framework, and name the accountable person for each role in section 5.
- Set the date for the first engagement in the calendar.
- Set the date for the 12-month CEOF Diagnostic re-assessment.
- Decide whether advisory support is wanted for the first six months (Ongoing Strategic Advisor). I raise this once, here.
