# Engagement framework template

> **What this is:** the final deliverable of a Consumer Engagement Design project, as a fill-in template. I complete it with the client; the client then owns and runs it. Fill in the [square brackets] and delete the guidance notes in italics. It is the organisation's document, so it is written in the organisation's name.
> **Sizes:** Focused delivers sections 1 to 8, with measures for the three lowest CEOF domains and a 90-day plan in place of sections 9 and 10. Standard and Full deliver all ten sections.

## 7. Safety and ethics

**Consent:** every participant receives a plain-language information sheet at least seven days ahead and gives written consent. Consent is checked aloud at the start of each session. [Location of the approved information sheet and consent form.]

**Privacy:** [what is collected; where it is stored; who can see it; when it is deleted; privacy policy reference]. Findings are reported as themes. No one is named or quoted without written agreement.

**Access:** access needs are asked at application and at confirmation, and are funded. [Standard provisions: captioning, interpreters, phone options, accessible venues.]

**Distress:** two people run every session. The distress protocol at [location] is followed. Support contacts are given at every session.

**Not clinical:** engagement sessions are not counselling and nothing said in them is medical advice.

**Honesty about limits:** every invitation states what is and is not open to influence.

**Research:** where engagement findings are to be published as research, ethics approval is obtained first by [role].

**Incidents and complaints:** [how they are recorded, who reviews them, how the participant hears the outcome].

*Company clients, add:*
- No product promotion and no product names in any engagement.
- Contact with consumers is at arm's length through [independent party]. [Company] staff do not attend consumer sessions.
- [Company] receives no information identifying an individual. Counts are not reported for groups smaller than ten.
- Funding is disclosed to participants in plain words before they agree to take part.
- Nothing is directed at encouraging anyone to ask for a prescription medicine.
- Support of patient organisations is reported as the Medicines Australia Code of Conduct requires.
