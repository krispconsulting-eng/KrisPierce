# Caregiver Wellbeing: current decisions

Read this file first. It overrides anything else in this repository where they conflict, including the lessons, the framework files, the website and the app.

Authority: the Caregiver Wellbeing decisions register dated 10 October 2026 (Google Drive) is the source. This file summarises it. If they differ, the register wins; fix this file.

Owner and final approver: Kris Pierce.
Last updated: 10 October 2026.

## Identity

- Public name: Caregiver Wellbeing. Operated by Kris Pierce Consulting. It is not an SCN2A Australia program.
- Contact: info@krispierce.com.au. The SCN2A Australia address must not appear anywhere.
- Tagline: "Space for YOU", written that way everywhere.
- Term: "caregiver" throughout.
- Audience: any unpaid caregiver, whether caring for a child, young person, adult or ageing parent. Do not write for parents of children or rare disease only.

## Program

- An eight-week Guided Journey.
- The method is "One small thing" (the One Small Thing method in documents for organisations). It replaces Tiny Steps and Small Supportive Actions. One action per week, under five minutes.
- No points, levels, streaks or badges for caregivers. The game code stays in reserve for other audiences. Do not delete it; do not show it to caregivers.
- No invented people, testimonials or lived-experience stories. Kris is writing the composite stories. Existing story text is placeholder only and must not be published.

## Launch scope (Carers Week, 11 to 17 October 2026)

- A top-level "Wellbeing" page on krispierce.com.au with register-your-interest, information on caregiving and balance, the Wheel marked as coming, and sponsorship marked "coming soon".
- No sponsor pricing, tiers or enquiry form. No assessment, enrolment or course pricing.
- Goes live as soon as it is checked: the form reaches Kris, the contact is correct, and the page works on a phone.

## Brand (Natural Coastal v1.2, brand folder v2.1)

- Colours: Deep ocean #14384A, Deep natural #4D7765, Coastal mist #A8C9D0, Soft sand #EADBC8, Warm paper #F8F7F2, White #FFFFFF. Accents (artwork only, never text): Coastal blue #A7C4D4, Sage #B7C8B1, Sunrise #E9B88B, Lilac #C9B7D6 (not in social graphics).
- Links use Deep natural. No black. No gradients or glows. No emoji.
- Type: Newsreader for headings, Hanken Grotesk for everything else.
- Logo: use the supplied files untouched (suite v1.1, locked). Never redraw or retype.
- Brand Library (formerly Media Catalogue) is the locked look.
- Relaxed on 10 October: text on photographs, uppercase labels, the approved watercolour strokes and the leaf sprig are permitted. Older blanket bans on these are superseded.

## Voice

Calm, plain, practical. UK English with Australian conventions. Sentence case. No em dashes, no exclamation marks. Speak to "you". Permission-giving, not instructive. No guilt, urgency, countdowns or scores. Health content states its limits and points to a GP.
