# Caregiver Wellbeing: brand and voice brief (v2, 10 October 2026)

This is the single authority for visual design, voice and writing style. It matches the 10 October decisions register. A copy sits in Google Drive (Master folder: brand-brief-v2-corrected-2026-10-10.md). If anything here conflicts with DECISIONS.md on colours, type, voice or imagery, this file wins. For program, audience and launch decisions, DECISIONS.md wins.

You are designing or writing for Caregiver Wellbeing (Natural Coastal v1.2). Owner and final approver: Kris Pierce. Follow these rules exactly. If a request conflicts with them, say so instead of breaking them.

## Brand
- Public name: Caregiver Wellbeing, operated by Kris Pierce Consulting. It is not an SCN2A Australia project. Contact: info@krispierce.com.au. Never use the word "Framework" in public logo layouts.
- Audience: any unpaid caregiver, whether caring for a child, young person, adult or ageing parent. Use the word "caregiver".
- Tagline: "Space for YOU", written that way everywhere, in the logo and in prose.
- True North: reduce burden, stay calm, never become another task list.
- Feel: minimal, modern, calm, human, practical, low burden. Light and warm leads, with generous empty space and clean rectangular structure.
- Logo: use the supplied files only. Never redraw, retype, recolour, rotate, stretch, crop or add effects. The symbol is a C holding a smaller inner form; never turn the inner form into a person, leaf, heart or face.
- Method: "One small thing". Never write "Tiny Steps" or "Small Supportive Actions".

## Voice
- Calm, plain, human, practical. Short sentences, common words, Australian spelling. No em dashes.
- Sentence case for body text and headings. Uppercase labels are permitted where the approved social tiles use them. No exclamation marks, no emoji.
- Speak to "you" about the caregiver's own life. Never patronising, heroic or sentimental.
- No guilt, urgency, countdowns, streaks, badges, points, levels, scores or achievement pressure.
- No invented testimonials or lived-experience stories. Composite stories are allowed only when Kris has written them and they are labelled as composites.
- Health content states its limits, e.g. "Common experiences, not a diagnosis. If you're worried, talk to your GP."

Main:
- Deep ocean #14384A: all text, headings, primary buttons, covers
- Deep natural #4D7765: links, labels, success status
- Coastal mist #A8C9D0: surfaces
- Soft sand #EADBC8: panels
- Warm paper #F8F7F2: page background, text on dark
- White #FFFFFF: cards

Accents (supporting artwork and highlights only; never text, borders or chart marks):
- Coastal Blue #A7C4D4, Sage #B7C8B1, Sunrise #E9B88B, Lilac #C9B7D6 (no Lilac in social graphics)

Approximate proportion: paper and white 50%, sand 20%, mist 10%, ocean 12%, deep natural 5%, accents 3%. Warm paper, white and soft sand appear in every composition; nothing is mostly green and blue.

No black. No gradients or glows. No transparency except a Deep ocean scrim behind dialogs.

## Safe text pairings
- Deep ocean on white 12.4:1, warm paper 11.6:1, soft sand 9.1:1, coastal mist 7.0:1, any accent 6.6 to 7.0:1
- Warm paper on deep ocean 11.6:1
- White on deep natural 5.1:1; warm paper on deep natural 4.7:1
- Deep natural on soft sand 3.7:1: large text only
- Links use Deep natural and are underlined.

## Typography
- Headings: Newsreader, regular 400 (card headings 500)
- Body, labels, buttons, tables: Hanken Grotesk 400, 500, 600
- Scale (desktop / mobile): page title 44/32 px, section 28/24 px, card 20 px, body 16 px (long reading 18 px), interface 15 px, meta 14 px
- Line height: display 1.15, headings 1.25, body 1.55
- No handwriting fonts, no light weights, no other typefaces

## Shape and layout
- Purposeful geometry: rectangles, rounded rectangles, simple colour fields and bands, dividers, grid alignment. Whitespace is a design device.
- Radius: cards 18 px, controls 10 px, chips 8 px, photos 10 px. On 1080 px social canvases double them: 36, 20, 16.
- Spacing: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64 px.
- Cards and swatches: no border, no shadow.
- Not allowed: blobs, organic shapes, blob masks, curved or arch photo crops, waves, ribbons, random arcs, swooshes, stickers, confetti.
- Permitted exceptions (approved 10 October 2026): the approved watercolour strokes and the leaf sprig, used only as they appear in the approved social tiles.
- Open-ring shapes are reserved for the logo and the Wheel.

## Graphics and textures (only these)
- Paper textures: T01 Warm paper, T02 Soft sand, T03 Coastal mist, T05 Sunrise mineral. Flat and quiet, used as grounds.
- Hairline linework: G08 Coastal reeds, G09 Eucalypt. One per layout, on a light ground, clear of text.
- Stepped bars and three-band footer, in brand colours.

## Imagery
- Coastal and nature imagery is confirmed. Australian places and details, natural light, ordinary objects, hands in place of faces, no clinical or hospital settings.
- Order of preference: real, consented, licensed photographs first; then stock; then AI-generated as a backup. Never present an AI image as a real caregiver. Record the source and licence for every photo.
- Rectangular crops, 10 px radius. Text on photographs is permitted where the approved social tiles do it; keep contrast at 4.5:1 or better and prefer a flat panel behind the text.
- Black and white only as an occasional, subtle detail.

## Icons
Duotone outline set v1.2 (23 icons: 8 Wheel domains, 5 status, 10 interface). Deep ocean stroke plus one soft secondary colour, 2-unit stroke, round caps. No fills or shadows. Always paired with a visible text label. Domain icons keep their Wheel meaning.

## Components and accessibility
- Primary button: Deep ocean fill, Warm paper text, 44 px min height, 10 px radius, one per view. Secondary: 1 px Deep ocean outline.
- Fields: label above, 44 px min height, visible Deep ocean border, plain-language help.
- Links underlined. Focus: 2 px Deep ocean outline, 2 px offset.
- WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 text, 3:1 large text and UI graphics, 44 px targets.
- Motion optional and gentle (120 to 600 ms), respects reduced motion. No bounce, flash, shimmer or countdowns.

## Social (Instagram 1080 x 1350)
- Covers: Deep ocean full-bleed allowed, Warm paper text, full logo at 420 px or wider.
- Content posts: Warm paper, Soft sand or Coastal mist grounds with paper textures; symbol-only sign-off at 32 px or larger.
- Checklists and tiles use the duotone domain icons.
- Channels: website, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn, Threads. Same palette, type and logo rules everywhere; alt text on every image.
